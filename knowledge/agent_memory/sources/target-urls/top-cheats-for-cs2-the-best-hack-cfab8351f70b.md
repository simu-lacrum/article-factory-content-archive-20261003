You may have heard of this project; it’s a cheat with an interesting history and a wide range of features.

Neverlose is a rage cheat geared more toward HvH (a game mode where cheaters play against cheaters). Projects like this are **more likely to be detected by VAC**, and I don’t recommend using it if you’re worried about your account. In that case, I recommend looking for external solutions like Cluster.

**Let’s briefly go over the pros:**

1.   Built-in skinchanger
2.   Rage features (relatively speaking; this will be important to some people)
3.   Responsive technical support
4.   User-friendly interface
5.   Beautiful visuals

The only downside I can point out is the price; the subscription is quite expensive.

## 3. Fatality

Another fairly old and well-known HvH cheat, aimed primarily at players who prefer to rage. I also don’t recommend it for people who want to stay off VAC’s radar.

Let’s briefly go over the pros:

1.   Fatality frequently delights its users with events like a wheel of fortune or Easter egg hunts right in the software menu
2.   Ability to load custom skins
3.   Affordable price compared to its direct competitor — Neverlose

However, the interface is completely clunky, and the stability of their driver is questionable: BSODs can occur on some systems.

Let me emphasize this again:

A Semirage cheat that has proven itself since the release of CS2, as the developers quickly provided workarounds for server checks and VACLive.

If you’re not aware, Semirage is a failed attempt to combine legit and rage. The result is something that isn’t as safe as a full-fledged legit, but also not as interesting as rage.

**Let’s briefly go over the pros:**

1.   For the price, they offer a pretty decent number of feature
2.   Stable performance across different systems

As for the cons, I’ll highlight the absolutely awful and clunky design of everything, as well as its questionable security against VAC. Over the past year, there have been no complaints from users about VAC bans, but knowing what the product looks like from the inside — this is unlikely to last forever.
