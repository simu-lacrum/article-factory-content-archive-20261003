I have been playing Dota with cheats for about 5 years and decided to compile a list of the best hacks based on my experience, as well as third-party reviews from users of all products.

## 1. Melonity.gg

Melonity is one of the oldest and most popular solutions on the market. If you see a cheater in your game, they are likely using this [**particular hack**](https://melonity.gg/en)(Melonity.gg).

The product positions itself as a premium solution and is closer to a full-fledged gaming assistant, with most of the innovations in the field coming from this project’s team. However, Melonity allows you to play in both styles: rage and legit.

Let’s briefly go over the pros:

1.   Auto dodge shells - forget about hooks hitting you. One of the best implementations.
2.   Low Poly Map - gives the map a new low-poly style. No one has been able to copy anything like it yet
3.   Maphack. So you always know where the enemy is. Very often, this is where new bypasses appear
4.   Unique implementation of skinchanger, which does not reduce FPS.

5. Over 200 scripts for each hero and for every game situation.

6. Design - the cheat interfaces fit perfectly into the game and match its style. For example, the build assistant or MetaPicker.

7. One of the best defenses against VAC. The cheat has been in the undertext for many years and recently received an updated humanizer from a _possible_ vac-live.

8. API available for creating custom scripts.

## 2. Umbrella

Umbrella is also not a newcomer, having been on the market for about nine years. The product is slightly more affordable in terms of price, literally a couple of dollars cheaper than Melonity, but the quality is also slightly lower. The main difference is in the humanizer; the one mentioned above from Melonity is the best on the market. And although users are not currently receiving vac-live bans, everything may change in the near future.

## 3. Divine

A relative newcomer to the market, less than 5 years old, but still worth mentioning. At one time, it released some very interesting visual scripts, which are now, however, commonplace.

It feels like they have stopped paying enough attention to hack. The subscription is also quite affordable, but I would not consider purchasing it, as with all the other top members listed below.

Briefly on the pros:

1.   Low price. However, the quality is commensurate with the price.
2.   A standard set of scripts for heroes that work well, but lasthit, dodger, and automatic illusions leave something to be desired.

## 5. Hake

The oldest of the currently available Dota hacks. A true legend, but one that has not kept up with the technological race between other projects. The price is slightly higher than the previous two, without any additional functionality, but it is quite stable.

Let me list some of its advantages:

1.   Stability. The scripts are old, nothing new, but they are well-established.
2.   Age. The fact that the project has been around for about 15 years speaks volumes.
3.   An extensive set of user scripts that can be downloaded for free from the website (the quality of these scripts can rarely be compared to those provided by the top sites, but those who seek will always find what they are looking for).
