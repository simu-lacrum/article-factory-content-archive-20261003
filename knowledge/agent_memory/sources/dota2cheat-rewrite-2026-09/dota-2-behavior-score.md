**To raise your Dota 2 behavior score, improve the conduct signals you can control.** Finish matches, prevent disconnects, stop replying to bait, and use one secure account consistently. Valve does not publish a formula that converts a fixed number of wins, commends, or games into a guaranteed score increase, so any paid “boost” or seven-day promise is marketing.

Use this as a four-week routine. It is deliberately boring: stable sessions produce better information than a grind made of abandons and arguments.

![Image 2: Dota 2 conduct summary with behavior and communication scores](https://dota2cheat.com/assets/dota2/dota2-fe0d7725c3377ee786edf5da.webp)

## Find the cause before changing the plan

Review your latest conduct summary and match history. Separate connection failures, intentional abandons, communication conflict, and gameplay reports. Each has a different fix. If the network or power is unreliable, do not queue until it is stable. Starting more matches cannot repair a disconnect pattern.

Also check practical constraints: queue only when you have time for a full game, close downloads that affect latency, and restart the client if it becomes unstable. These steps do not promise a score movement; they prevent avoidable negative events.

## Use a low-conflict communication rule

1.   State one plan in the draft, then keep it short.
2.   Use pings for information, not sarcasm or punishment.
3.   Mute a player as soon as a conversation becomes personal.
4.   Do not debate blame while the game is active.
5.   After the match, leave the argument instead of carrying it into the next queue.

A calm loss with normal participation is not the same input as an abandon or a match spent attacking teammates. Winning helps the experience, but behavior score is not simply a win-rate metric.

## Choose sessions you can finish

Use a small hero pool and a role you can complete without forcing a complicated experiment. Avoid queueing while tired, angry, or distracted by a call. Two finished games followed by a break are more useful than six rushed games that end in an abandon or a reportable argument.

Do not switch to a purchased or “fresh” account. Valve has linked smurf accounts to associated main accounts; read our [smurf-ban recovery guide](https://dota2cheat.com/guides/dota-2-smurf-ban-guide) before treating account rotation as a solution.

## A four-week routine

| When | Record | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before queue | Connection, time, mood | Skip the queue if any is unstable. |
| During draft | Role and one plan | Communicate once, then play. |
| During game | Mute triggers and abandons | Protect completion over arguments. |
| After game | Trigger and break | Stop after two tilted games. |
| Weekly | Conduct summary and history | Change one habit, not everything. |
![Image 4: Dota 2 behavior score recovery checklist](https://dota2cheat.com/assets/dota2/dota2-c9ebb8c608b079fe0fd1c82e.webp)

## Do not chase shortcuts

Do not buy commends, account boosting, queue manipulation, or a “clean” profile. A coordinated exchange turns feedback into a target and may create additional reports or credential theft. A temporary number change is not recovery if the same conduct continues.

Likewise, do not expect a hidden queue, hero choice, or win streak to reset the score. Track inputs rather than checking after every game. Conduct summaries can update in batches, and daily checking creates superstition rather than evidence.

## Make the next session easier to finish

Remove friction before you queue. Close software that competes for bandwidth, update the game outside your planned session, and choose a time when you can stay at the computer. If a crash or disconnect happens, record it and fix the underlying problem before starting another match. Do not use a network trick, account swap, or paid service to turn a technical problem into someone else's conduct signal.

When a teammate is frustrating, use one factual call, then mute. You are not required to win an argument to finish a match. If you notice that your own messages are becoming sarcastic or repetitive, stop typing and take the break you planned. The [private-lobby practice guide](https://dota2cheat.com/guides/dota-2-lobby-cheat-commands) is useful for learning a hero outside a high-pressure queue. Keep this routine for a full review window instead of changing tactics after one disappointing summary.

## When progress stalls

Reduce volume before changing the entire strategy. Re-check connection stability, mute earlier, and leave a longer gap between sessions. Keep notes private and factual: date, completed game, abandon, conflict trigger, and what you changed. Never publish teammate names or personal data.
