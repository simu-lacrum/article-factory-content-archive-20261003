**A Dota 2 smurf ban is not fixed by buying another account.** Valve has said that smurfing is not welcome, that alternate accounts can be linked to a player's main account, and that related accounts may receive penalties. The safest response is to stop using an alternate account for matchmaking manipulation, secure the account you own, and return to normal play.

This guide explains the policy boundary and the actions you can verify. It does not provide a way to hide account links, imitate a beginner, or evade enforcement.

![Image 2: Dota 2 account and matchmaking policy guide](https://dota2cheat.com/assets/dota2-hero.webp)

## What Valve means by smurfing

In its official smurfing notice, Valve described action against 90,000 smurf accounts and said associated main accounts could also be affected. The important point is account use, not a magic number of matches. Valve does not publish a “safe” win rate, hardware profile, rank gap, or match count that makes deliberate manipulation acceptable.

A new player learning Dota 2, a family member using their own Steam account, and a player deliberately placing an experienced account below its established level are different situations. Valve decides how a specific account is classified. A seller's label such as “aged,” “calibrated,” or “clean” does not change that decision.

## Why a purchased account makes the problem worse

An account bought from a marketplace can have an unknown ownership history, previous reports, payment disputes, or recovery claims from its original owner. You cannot audit every login, match, or device associated with it. Sharing credentials with the seller also creates a separate security risk.

Do not follow advice about changing hardware, rotating IP addresses, losing early games, or keeping a particular percentage of wins. Those tactics are speculative evasion and may connect more accounts to the same pattern. They also leave you without a reliable account-recovery story if Steam Support asks for ownership evidence.

## What to do after a warning or penalty

1.   **Stop alternate-account matchmaking.** Do not queue a second profile while deciding what to do.
2.   **Secure the account you own.** Change the Steam password from a trusted device, confirm the recovery email, and review Steam Guard and authorised sessions.
3.   **Read the exact notice.** Save the visible message, affected account, game, and date. Do not infer a hidden reason from forum screenshots.
4.   **Use official support only.** If an appeal or ownership question is available, submit concise facts through Steam Support. Never send a password, mobile code, or recovery code to a third party.
5.   **Remove untrusted software.** Uninstall third-party loaders and review the security guidance in our [Dota 2 detection guide](https://dota2cheat.com/guides/dota-2-cheat-detection).

Support may not disclose detection details or reverse an enforcement action. A person promising a guaranteed unban, “clean flag,” or hardware reset is selling a claim, not access to Valve's internal records.

## Smurf status and behavior score are different

A behavior score reflects conduct signals such as communication and reports. Smurf enforcement concerns account and matchmaking misuse. Improving communication cannot erase an account-link decision, and changing accounts cannot repair a conduct history. Use the [behavior-score recovery plan](https://dota2cheat.com/guides/dota-2-behavior-score) for the conduct side of the problem.

## A realistic one-account recovery plan

Choose the account you own, keep its credentials private, and queue only when you have enough time and a stable connection to finish. Use a small hero pool while you rebuild consistent habits. You do not need to lose intentionally, pretend to be new, or chase a winning streak. A completed, ordinary match is a better signal than a forced experiment.

Before each session, check your connection and leave enough time for a full game. During the match, keep communication short and mute escalating conversations. After a difficult game, take a break instead of changing accounts. Review the same inputs each week: abandons, disconnects, reports, and whether you entered a match while tilted.

## What recovery cannot promise

No public calculator can predict when a score, restriction, or matchmaking experience will change. Valve can change systems and apply decisions using information that is not public. Treat every date and result as an observation, not a guarantee.
