**Dota 2 smurf ban** advice should not be a guide to choosing a less detectable account. Valve has explicitly said smurfing is not welcome and has linked smurf accounts back to their associated main accounts. Buying an older profile, changing hardware or adjusting early-match behavior does not turn evasion into compliant play.

The durable response is to stop using alternate accounts for matchmaking manipulation, secure the main account and rebuild normal play on one identity.

## What Valve calls smurfing

Smurfing generally describes using an alternate account to play below a player's established skill or otherwise manipulate matchmaking. Shared household access, a genuinely new player and a returning user can look different from deliberate rank manipulation, but only Valve controls enforcement and account classification.

In [Smurfing is Not Welcome in Dota](https://www.dota2.com/newsentry/3692442542242977036), Valve announced action against 90,000 smurf accounts and said associated main accounts could receive penalties ranging from behavior-score adjustments to permanent bans.

## Why a purchased account is not a solution

An account sold as “aged,” “calibrated” or “safe” may be stolen, recovered by its original owner, linked to previous misconduct or already under review. The buyer cannot verify its complete history. Account trading also creates credential and payment disputes outside normal support.

Advice about ideal match counts, win rates or hardware fingerprints is speculative and encourages evasion. Valve does not publish a threshold that makes a smurf acceptable.

## What to do after a warning or penalty

1.   Stop using alternate accounts for lower-skill matchmaking.
2.   Secure the main Steam account and remove unknown sessions.
3.   Review account notifications and official support information.
4.   Do not pay anyone who claims they can remove an enforcement action.
5.   Play normally on one account and allow the system to collect legitimate match history.

A warning is not an invitation to rotate to another profile. Repeated evasion can connect more accounts to the same pattern.

## Behavior score and smurf status are different

Behavior score reflects conduct signals such as communication and reports; smurf enforcement concerns account and matchmaking misuse. Improving one does not erase the other. No public calculator can promise a specific recovery rate because Valve does not publish the full model.

## A legitimate account reset plan

Choose one account, secure it, remove unauthorized software, and play a stable pool of roles without abusive communication or deliberate abandonment. Keep expectations realistic: matchmaking and conduct signals need time, and a short winning streak is not an account reset.

The [Dota 2 account policy and tool guides](https://cheatsgaming.com/games/dota-2) collects related ban, VAC and behavior articles. None of them can override Valve's enforcement or guarantee an appeal outcome.

## Separate an appeal from an evasion service

Use only official account notifications and Steam Support routes to understand an action. A third party that sells a “clean flag,” hardware reset or guaranteed unban cannot alter Valve's internal account record. Sharing credentials with that service can create a second security incident.

When contacting support, provide concise facts: the account, visible message, approximate date and any relevant ownership evidence requested through the official form. Do not send passwords or mobile codes. Support may not disclose detection details, and a response that withholds those details is not proof that an outside seller knows more.

The recovery goal is stable, policy-compliant use of one account. It is not a sequence of experiments designed to discover which signal connected the accounts.

Friends and household members should use their own accounts and secure credentials. If several people share a computer, sign out fully and keep account protection enabled. Shared hardware does not make rank manipulation acceptable, but clear account ownership reduces confusion when reviewing sessions or contacting support.

A legitimate returning player should simply use the account they own and allow matchmaking to recalibrate through normal play. There is no need to imitate a beginner, lose intentionally or buy a profile with an invented history.

Normal play is the recovery method.
