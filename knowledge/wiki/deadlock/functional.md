## Functional

*   ### Aimbot

Configurable aim assistance for targeting enemy heroes. Available settings may include aim area, field of view and smoothing.

*   ### ESP

Displays configurable information about enemy heroes and other supported game objects through an on-screen overlay.

*   ### Hero Combo

Executes configured sequences of abilities and attacks for supported heroes. Availability may depend on the current game and product version.

*   ### Humanizer

Adjusts the speed, smoothing and behaviour of supported aim functions. It does not guarantee that automated actions will be undetectable.

*   ### Auto Parry

Automates parry timing for supported melee interactions. Results may be affected by latency and game updates.

*   ### Anti Parry

Adjusts the timing of supported attacks and combinations to make them less predictable for an opponent’s parry.

*   ### Souls Aimbot

Provides aim assistance when targeting Souls, helping users interact with them more consistently.

*   ### FOV Changer

Allows the supported field-of-view value to be adjusted for a wider or narrower view of the battlefield.

*   ### Dodger

Automatically dodge enemy abilities.
