## What are DEADLOCK cheats?

Deadlock cheats are unofficial tools or automations aimed at a game that combines third-person shooting with lanes, heroes, abilities, souls, objectives, and team movement. That mix makes generic “aimbot and ESP” lists incomplete. Auto-Parry can refer to reacting to an incoming charged melee attack, while Anti-Parry usually means changing the player’s own aim to make a heavy attack harder to counter. A FOV Changer alters the camera field of view and should be checked separately during normal movement, aiming, zoom states, and on ultrawide displays. An aim feature may also need to account for projectile travel, movement, or hero abilities, while an information feature may claim to show heroes, health, souls, cooldowns, minions, or objectives. Readers should check the exact game build, activation conditions, feature limits, and whether the evidence is a current capture or an old marketing asset. Deadlock remains in development, so interface and technical changes can make descriptions stale quickly. Valve announced an initial anti-cheat detection system in September 2024 and explicitly described it as an evolving system. No seller can turn that changing environment into a permanent “safe” or “undetected” guarantee.
