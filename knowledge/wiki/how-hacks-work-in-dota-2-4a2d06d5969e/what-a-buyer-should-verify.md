## What a buyer should verify

*   A recent status date tied to the current Dota 2 build.
*   Exact feature scope instead of broad “full protection” promises.
*   A transparent access period, price and update policy.
*   A known delivery channel and a written support path.
*   A direct warning that third-party cheating can lead to a permanent ban.
