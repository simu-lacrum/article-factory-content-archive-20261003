## The main Dota 2 cheat layers

Most products combine three layers. The first is information: cooldowns, ward clues, unit states or other data presented in an overlay. The second is assistance: target selection, cast timing or alerts. The third is automation: a fixed sequence reacts to a condition and sends several inputs. A feature list becomes useful only when it states which layer each function belongs to.

Read the labels carefully. “Map awareness” may mean a legal reminder based on visible events, or it may mean access to information hidden by fog of war. “Combo helper” may be a visual order list, or it may execute actions.
