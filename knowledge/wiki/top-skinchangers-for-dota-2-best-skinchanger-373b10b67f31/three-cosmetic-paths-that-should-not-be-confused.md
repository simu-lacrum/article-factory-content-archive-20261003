## Three cosmetic paths that should not be confused

Official inventory cosmetics are items recognized by the game and platform. Workshop submissions are creator assets reviewed through Valve's publishing pipeline. A third-party skin changer modifies or replaces what the local client presents. Those paths have different ownership, visibility and policy implications.

Valve's [official Dota 2 Workshop requirements](https://www.dota2.com/workshop/requirements) describe the supported creator-submission path; they are not an endorsement of injected or locally patched cosmetic tools.
