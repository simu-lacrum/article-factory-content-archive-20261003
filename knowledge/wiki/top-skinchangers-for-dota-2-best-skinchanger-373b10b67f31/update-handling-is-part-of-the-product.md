## Update handling is part of the product

Cosmetic definitions and game files change. A maintained tool should show a recent test date and pause downloads when compatibility is uncertain. Silent replacement of files after a patch can lead to crashes, missing models or repeated repair downloads.

Our [Dota 2 skin changer risk checklist](https://cheatsgaming.com/games/dota-2/dota-2-skin-changer-2026-risks-and-tools-baeb9ed150af) explains what to verify before and after an update.
