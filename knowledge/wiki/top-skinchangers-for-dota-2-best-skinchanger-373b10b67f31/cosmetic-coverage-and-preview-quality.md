## Cosmetic coverage and preview quality

Check whether a tool covers hero sets, individual slots, couriers, wards, terrain, weather, announcers or interface elements. The listing should state exclusions. Search, filters and a before-and-after preview are more useful than a raw count of assets.

Also ask whether changes are local-only. If other players cannot see them, the product is a client-side visual customization rather than an inventory item. The page should say that plainly.
