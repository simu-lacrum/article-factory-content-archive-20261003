### **Step 1: Verify that you are on the official website**

Before downloading anything, inspect the domain carefully. Typosquatted websites can copy a real project’s logo, screenshots and text while replacing the download with a malicious file.

A few seconds of verification can prevent a serious mistake:

*   read the domain character by character —**cluster.center**;
*   do not trust a page only because it uses the same logo;
*   bookmark the official page after you have verified it;
*   use the download button inside the official account area rather than a third-party mirror.

If two pages claim to be the official loader and you cannot tell which one is legitimate, do not download either until the provider confirms it.
