## What features do modern cheats have?

There have always been very few cheats for Deadlock, and now, in fact, there’s only one left — [**Cluster (link active)**](https://cluster.center/en/deadlock) — which I’ve been using almost since the game’s release.

This is due to the complexity of creating them. While a simple aimbot is enough for FPS games, Deadlock requires a customized approach because of the huge number of mechanics on top of the FPS gameplay.

I won’t go into details, but I’ll give a general overview of the current capabilities. A little later, I’ll dedicate an entire article to the functionality of cheats in Deadlock.
