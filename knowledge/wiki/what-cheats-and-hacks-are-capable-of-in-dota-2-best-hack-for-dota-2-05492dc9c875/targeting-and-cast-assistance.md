## Targeting and cast assistance

Targeting aids may select a nearby enemy, prioritize a configured unit or draw a suggested cast position. Prediction features try to account for movement and projectile travel. Their result still depends on latency, vision, spell behavior, immunity, dispels and the player's configuration.

A cast assistant cannot know every strategic exception. Automatically using a disable on the first valid target may waste it before a more important enemy appears. Fast execution is not the same as correct decision-making.
