## Hero scripts and combo logic

Hero scripts connect conditions to actions. A script might order several abilities and items, react to a channel, or apply a defensive item when a condition is met. The quality depends on the logic, supported hero build and how well the author handles edge cases.

Patch changes can invalidate item names, timings, cast ranges and talent assumptions. The [complete Dota 2 scripts terminology guide](https://cheatsgaming.com/games/dota-2/dota-2-cheats-scripts-explained-2025-all-about-dota-hacks-82ff479af22d) explains the difference between alerts, macros, rules and full automation.
