### Композиция

- no centered collage of unrelated objects;
- no more than one hero;
- no tiny decorative details in the headline zone;
- no accidental tangencies between object and text safe zone;
- no symmetrical layout unless front-facing hardware is the concept;
- no generic diagonal speed lines.
