### 25.1. Измеренные токены сайта

Проверено на desktop 1280 × 720 и mobile 390 × 844, 8 сентября 2026 года:

- publisher electric blue: `#2B58FF`;
- publisher deep blue: `#001FD4`;
- charcoal / ink: `#0E0E10`;
- paper / off-white: `#F2F2F2`;
- status green: `#6DB33F`;
- optional pale blue: `#A6D8FF`;
- display typography: Orbitron Black / 900 or a metrically close square techno grotesk;
- body typography: Inter Regular / Medium;
- labels and controls: IBM Plex Mono Medium, uppercase, slight positive tracking;
- control radius: 9 px; panel radius: 14 px; outer frame radius: 20 px;
- cards use a thin 20% charcoal rule and no decorative shadow.
