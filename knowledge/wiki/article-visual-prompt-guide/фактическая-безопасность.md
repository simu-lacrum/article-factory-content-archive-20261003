### Фактическая безопасность

- no fake statistics;
- no invented interface presented as real;
- no current patch number unless verified and required;
- no price, ban rate, detection status or guarantee;
- no operational anti-cheat bypass diagram;
- no credential, exploit, injector or evasion workflow.
