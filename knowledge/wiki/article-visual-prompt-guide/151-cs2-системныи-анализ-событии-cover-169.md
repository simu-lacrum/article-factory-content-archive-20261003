### 15.1. CS2: системный анализ событий, cover 16:9

```text
Model target: Nano Banana Pro / gemini-3-pro-image. Style routing: generated asset #2, branch A — tactile industrial product poster. Brand accent: Cluster #635FD5 on one status element only, about 3–5% of the frame.

Create a 16:9 landscape hero cover for an editorial technology article about how a competitive-game system evaluates a sequence of match events rather than relying on one isolated moment.

Editorial thesis: reliable analysis comes from ordered context. Represent this as a compact industrial sorting hub built from matte charcoal and translucent acid-yellow modules. One stream of small neutral event tokens enters from the left, passes through three clearly separated chambers, and exits as one calm white status light on the right. The machine is the only hero.

Compose for a 2K master. Reserve the left upper area from x=6–43% and y=8–48% as a clean off-white headline zone with almost no detail. Place the machine on the right half, occupying about 55% of the frame; allow a controlled crop at the right edge. Three hierarchy levels maximum.

Camera: high three-quarter view, close to isometric, 45 mm equivalent, deep focus, no wide-angle distortion. Materials: matte soft-touch charcoal plastic, translucent yellow cast acrylic with correct wall thickness, a small amount of brushed steel. Background: warm off-white studio floor, no room architecture.

Palette: off-white #EFEFEA background, charcoal #1E1E1D machine body, acid yellow #E8E34A transparent processing modules, one Cluster-violet #635FD5 status block covering about 4% of the frame. Do not spread violet into the background or global light. Lighting: large hard-soft source from upper left, clean long shadows, controlled acrylic refraction.

Typography mode: art-first. Leave the headline zone completely empty: no text, glyphs, numbers, pseudo-labels or logos.

Reference roles: use the yellow industrial-hub reference only for high-angle process readability; use the black-object-on-red reference only for confident hero scale. Do not copy their objects, text, brand marks or exact layout.

The result must feel like a photographed industrial-design prototype for a modern tech magazine, not a game screenshot. No CS2 logo, maps, weapons, soldiers, hackers, code rain, neon cyan, HUD overlays, fake detection rates, charts or extra machines. If constraints conflict, prioritize thesis, one-hero hierarchy, headline safe zone, physical plausibility, exact brand accent, then surface detail.
```
