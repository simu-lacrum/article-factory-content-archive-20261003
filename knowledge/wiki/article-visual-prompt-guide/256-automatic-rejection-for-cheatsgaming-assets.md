### 25.6. Automatic rejection for CheatsGaming assets

Reject the result if it:

- looks like generic purple cyberpunk art with no paper/ink/blue editorial structure;
- uses a fake dashboard, loader, store card, price or detection-status indicator;
- copies the CheatsGaming logo or sets the article headline as a logo;
- makes green a decorative fill instead of a tiny status signal;
- uses more than one dominant hero or more than one main headline;
- loses the headline or thesis in a 390 px-wide crop;
- lets publisher blue replace the required Cluster/Melonity mapped field in a product-led B asset;
- adds bloom, glitch, code rain, hooded figures, weapon glamour or esports-poster effects.
