### 25.5. Prompt additions

Every CheatsGaming generated prompt must explicitly contain:

- `Publisher identity: CheatsGaming editorial system`;
- the four core publisher colors and their roles;
- Orbitron-like headline, Inter body only if a secondary caption is truly required, and IBM Plex Mono only for one exact micro-label;
- one sentence describing the 40/60 or 45/55 split and mobile-safe crop;
- one sentence forbidding copied logo, fake product UI, generic cyberpunk, neon hacker imagery and random microtext;
- the normal product mapped-color rule and the global generated index.
