### 15.5. Troubleshooting: бытовой натюрморт, 16:9

```text
Model target: Nano Banana Pro / gemini-3-pro-image. Style routing: generated asset #6, branch A — tactile domestic product still life. Brand accent: none unless the article explicitly names Cluster or Melonity.

Create a 16:9 2K editorial cover about diagnosing a persistent loading problem before reinstalling everything.

Tell the story through one quiet top-down still life on a brushed stainless-steel desk: a phone with one large acid-yellow loading ring, an unplugged cable lying one centimeter from the port, a used glass and a folded note. The phone is the hero; every prop must support the idea of an interrupted process.

Reserve the left upper quadrant as a low-detail headline zone. Place the phone right of center, rotated exactly 8 degrees clockwise. Use hard late-afternoon sunlight through blinds, creating two long diagonal shadows. Keep the scene 85% cool gray and steel, with yellow only on the phone screen.

Art-first mode: no readable text anywhere, no random interface labels. The phone screen contains only one geometrically clean loading ring.

Use the metal-table reference only for camera, restrained storytelling and light. Do not reproduce its arrangement, incoming-call UI or headline.

No hands, people, gaming equipment, RGB lights, coffee-shop branding, messy pile of objects, fake error codes, logos or dramatic smoke. If constraints conflict, prioritize interrupted-process meaning, one-phone hierarchy, empty headline zone, physical light and contacts, then prop detail.
```
