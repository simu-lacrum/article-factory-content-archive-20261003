### 25.4. Product color merge

- CS2 and Deadlock use Cluster violet `#635FD5`. In A it remains a 3–8% semantic accent. In B it replaces the warm reference's yellow/amber field and occupies roughly 35–70% of the frame. CheatsGaming blue may remain a secondary 10–25% editorial frame or panel.
- Dota 2 uses Melonity pink `#FF1469` under the same A/B rules. CheatsGaming blue stays secondary and must not neutralize the pink field.
- For publisher-level, multi-game topics, `#2B58FF` may be the largest publisher field only when no product is the visual subject. If Cluster or Melonity is named or pictured, its mapped color regains the required role.
- Never recolor `#635FD5`, `#FF1469` or `#2B58FF` to a merely similar hue. Write the exact hex values in the prompt.
