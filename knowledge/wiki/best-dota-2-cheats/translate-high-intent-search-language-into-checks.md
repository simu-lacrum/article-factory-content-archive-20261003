## Translate high-intent search language into checks

Searches such as “undetected Dota 2 cheat”, “safe Dota 2 hack” and “zero risk Dota 2 cheat” signal a buyer who wants confidence before payment. Treat those phrases as a request for current evidence: build date, module scope, update history, support and clear access terms. They are search language, not a product certification, so the live listing should explain the conditions behind its status wording.
