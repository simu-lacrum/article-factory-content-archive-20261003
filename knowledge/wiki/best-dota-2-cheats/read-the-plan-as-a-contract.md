## Read the plan as a contract

Before checkout, confirm the currency, duration, final price, activation method and when access time starts. Read the downtime and refund terms. If the product is unavailable during an update, does the clock stop, extend or continue? Do not infer an answer from a promotion.

Automatic delivery can reduce waiting, but it does not replace support. Confirm where activation occurs, whether access is tied to an account or device, and which channel handles a rejected key. Never send a Steam password, recovery code or browser cookie to “support”.
