## Melonity Safety Modules

*   AntiVAC — Developer-supplied anti-cheat protection module; it does not guarantee undetected use.
*   Spoofer — Changes supported device identifiers according to the developer’s description.
*   Humanizer — Makes supported automated actions appear more natural; it cannot guarantee account safety.
