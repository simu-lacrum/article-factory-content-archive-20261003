## Dota 2 Maphack & ESP

*   Jungle Maphack — Displays supported enemy activity in the fog of war when abilities are used.
*   Roshan ESP — Shows supported Roshan activity and health information.
*   Teleport Preview — Displays available information about enemy teleport movement.
*   Ward Tracker — Displays supported enemy ward information on the map.
