## Vision and map-state drills

Vision toggles can help review ward lines and spell ranges inside the controlled lobby. Creep-spawn and game-start controls can reproduce lane timing faster. These tools are valuable because the environment is explicit and consensual.

Do not confuse all-vision practice with a maphack in matchmaking. One is a lobby option enabled by the host; the other claims access to information ordinary players should not receive.
