## The difference between legal commands and real cheats

Before we move on to the list of commands, it’s important to draw a clear line. The game hacking community boasts numerous programs, scripts, and macros that provide an advantage in matchmaking like [**this one**](https://melonity.gg/), not in the lobby. These include:

1.   Autocast scripts (for example, the famous Invoker cheat in Dota 2 for instant casting).
2.   Maphacks (programs that reveal the fog of war).
3.   Software for automatically last-hitting creeps (auto-last-hitting) or dodging enemy abilities.

However, there are also Dota 2 console and chat cheats that have been officially added by the developers themselves. These can only be enabled in custom lobbies. They are designed for testing bugs, checking damage, practicing complex mechanics (for example, the Dota 2 Armlet Abuse cheat), and simply for fun playing with Dota 2 cheats against bots. It is impossible to get banned for using them, as they only work in a closed “sandbox” where you are the host.
