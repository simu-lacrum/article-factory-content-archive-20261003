## Gold, levels and item testing

Use the gold and level commands to reach a planned timing without playing an entire match. Item commands can build a specific inventory for damage or survivability tests. Record the hero level, talents, items and target armor so a later run is comparable.

A good drill changes one variable at a time. If you add levels, items and neutral bonuses together, you will not know which change produced the result.
