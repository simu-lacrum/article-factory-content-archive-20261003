## Why are these cheats useful in practice?

Here are a few examples of how experienced Dota 2 players use cheats in the lobby:

Invoker Practice: Turn on -wtf mode and practice unleashing a 5–7 ability burst in a couple of seconds using Blink Dagger and Refresher Orb. Your goal is to do this so quickly that your muscle memory becomes automatic.

Armlet Toggling Practice: Buy an Armlet of Mordiggian, go under an enemy tower or to Roshan, and practice the Dota 2 Armlet Abuse cheat, turning the item on and off between attacks to survive at 1 HP.

Ward Spotting: Use -allvision and a flying courier to find hidden tree textures where you can place an Observer Ward so the enemy can’t take it out with a standard Sentry.

Creep Stacks: Test the stacking mechanics (pulls) on multiple camps at once using illusions (for example, on Naga Siren or Terrorblade) and the -spawnneutrals command.
