## Cooldown, health and mana resets

Refresh and respawn commands shorten repetition. Test the same spell sequence from the same position, reset resources, then run it again. Unlimited-mana or no-cooldown modes are useful for learning order and range, but turn them off before testing realistic timing.

Practice first at normal speed. A drill that only works with artificial cooldowns can create habits that fail in a real match.
