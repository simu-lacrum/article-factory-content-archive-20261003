## Turn commands into a repeatable routine

*   Write the starting state in one short checklist.
*   Run five to ten repetitions with one goal.
*   Reset to the same health, mana and position.
*   Record failures, not only the fastest attempt.
*   Finish with normal cooldowns and visibility.
