## Spawn heroes and controlled targets

Create an enemy or allied hero to test interactions that Demo Hero does not make convenient. Add items and levels to the target, then repeat the setup. Hero internal names can differ from display names, so console autocomplete helps when a command rejects a name.

For programmable bot behavior rather than manual commands, read the [official Dota 2 bot-scripting workflow](https://cheatsgaming.com/games/dota-2/how-to-write-your-own-script-for-dota-2-on-any-hero-72043d903e1e).
