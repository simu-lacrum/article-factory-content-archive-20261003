## Use a low-conflict communication rule

Keep messages short, factual and related to the next action. Do not debate blame during the match. Mute escalating conversations early rather than answering every provocation. Pings should communicate information, not punish a teammate.

A simple rule works: one useful call, no personal criticism, then silence if the exchange becomes hostile. This protects focus and reduces the number of interactions that can generate reports.
