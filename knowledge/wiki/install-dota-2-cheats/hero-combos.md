#### Hero Combos

The Combo section is dedicated to automating the use of abilities and items for each character in the game. Melonity combos take into account a lot of information and game scenarios: from cooldowns of your teammates’ and opponents’ abilities to the amount of HP and mana.

However, combos can also be customized, so pay more attention to this section. Although they work quite intuitively, some players still encounter problems when using them, so feel free to contact Melonity technical support for any questions. You can find contacts directly on the website or in the launcher.

**Here you can find the very ones:**

*   Invoker Combos: Automatic sunstrike and more
*   Combo on Arc Warden

And so on, literally for every character in the game.
