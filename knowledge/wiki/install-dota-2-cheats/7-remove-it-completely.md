## 7. Remove it completely

At the end of access, use the documented uninstaller. Verify the folders, services, startup entries and drivers the installer listed. Repairing Dota files alone may not remove components installed elsewhere in Windows. If you ever supplied credentials to an untrusted program, change them and review active sessions immediately.
