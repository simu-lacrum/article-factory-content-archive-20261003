## 1. Open Melonity and create an account

Open [melonity.gg/en](https://dota2cheat.com/go/melonity) and choose **Create account**. Use an email address you control and a unique password. Do not reuse your Steam password. If the page redirects to a different domain or asks for unrelated credentials, stop and return to the authenticated account flow.
