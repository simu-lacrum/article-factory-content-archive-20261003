### Before Installing Anything

Don’t rush into downloading.

Most failed installations start the same way: someone searches for “free Dota 2 cheat,” opens a reposted file, skips the official website, and then wonders why the installer looks suspicious.

Before installing any Dota 2 cheat, check the following key points:

*   Are you on the official melonity.gg website?
*   Is the product page specifically for Dota 2?
*   Are you logged into your account?
*   Are the instructions taken from an official source?
*   Are you avoiding random archives, mirrors, and third-party launchers?

This is more important than many people realize. When using Dota 2 cheats, the first mistake is usually not the configuration, but downloading from an unsuitable source.
