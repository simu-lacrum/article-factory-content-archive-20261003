## Official cosmetics and unofficial replacement tools

Owned Dota items and approved Workshop submissions pass through supported Valve systems. A local skin changer may replace assets, patch files or interact with the running client. The visual result can be similar while the delivery method is fundamentally different.

Use [Valve's current Dota 2 Workshop specifications](https://www.dota2.com/workshop/requirements) to understand the supported creator path. Do not treat a reference to “Workshop skins” on a third-party page as proof that its installer follows that path.

A skin changer changes the local visual experience, not the official market value of an account.
