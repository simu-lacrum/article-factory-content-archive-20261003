## Account risk and VAC claims

[Steam Support's VAC modification warning](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/571A-97DA-70E9-FF74) tells users to be cautious when installing custom skins or scripts and says third-party modifications providing an advantage are treated as cheats. Valve alone controls enforcement.

“Client-side,” “inventory-free” and “stream-proof” do not mean “undetectable.” They describe presentation or architecture, not the future response of anti-cheat systems.
