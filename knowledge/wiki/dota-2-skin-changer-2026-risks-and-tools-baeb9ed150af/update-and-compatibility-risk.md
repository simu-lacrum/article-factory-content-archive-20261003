## Update and compatibility risk

Dota patches can move or replace assets and change how the client validates files. A skin changer may break models, trigger crashes or leave stale components after an update. The product should move to an updating state until its developer retests the current build.

Check the exact operating system and game version. “Windows supported” is incomplete if the tool has only been checked on one edition or architecture.
