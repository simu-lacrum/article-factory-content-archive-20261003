## Privacy and maintenance are separate questions

Anti-cheat architecture is only one part of the decision. Read the vendor's current documentation for startup behavior, update requirements, driver or service removal, supported operating systems, and what happens when the component is disabled. A broad system permission can be a security and compatibility concern even when no game penalty occurs. Conversely, a smaller client component is not proof that coverage is weak.

Record the source and date for each claim. Riot and Valve can change services, clients, and enforcement workflows after this article is published. Revisit the official Vanguard support page and Valve's VAC guidance instead of treating an old comparison as a current specification.

Do not compare products by a single word such as “kernel” or “cloud.” Ask what the component does at startup, which game it protects, how updates are delivered, and which support page explains removal. A precise question produces a useful answer; a broad label produces another marketing argument.
