## Keep the categories separate

*   **Architecture:** what the vendor publicly says is installed or checked.
*   **Policy:** what the game rules classify as an advantage.
*   **Enforcement:** what account message or restriction a user sees.
*   **Evidence:** whether a claim has a dated, primary source.

Do not use one category to infer another. A kernel component does not prove complete coverage; a user who is not yet banned does not prove a tool is invisible; and one public ban does not reveal the entire detection system.
