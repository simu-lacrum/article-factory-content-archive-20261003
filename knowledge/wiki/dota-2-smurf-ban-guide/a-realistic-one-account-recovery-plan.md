## A realistic one-account recovery plan

Choose the account you own, keep its credentials private, and queue only when you have enough time and a stable connection to finish. Use a small hero pool while you rebuild consistent habits. You do not need to lose intentionally, pretend to be new, or chase a winning streak. A completed, ordinary match is a better signal than a forced experiment.

Before each session, check your connection and leave enough time for a full game. During the match, keep communication short and mute escalating conversations. After a difficult game, take a break instead of changing accounts. Review the same inputs each week: abandons, disconnects, reports, and whether you entered a match while tilted.
