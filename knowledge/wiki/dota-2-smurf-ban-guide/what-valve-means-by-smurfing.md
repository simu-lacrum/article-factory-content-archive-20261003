## What Valve means by smurfing

In its official smurfing notice, Valve described action against 90,000 smurf accounts and said associated main accounts could also be affected. The important point is account use, not a magic number of matches. Valve does not publish a “safe” win rate, hardware profile, rank gap, or match count that makes deliberate manipulation acceptable.

A new player learning Dota 2, a family member using their own Steam account, and a player deliberately placing an experienced account below its established level are different situations. Valve decides how a specific account is classified. A seller's label such as “aged,” “calibrated,” or “clean” does not change that decision.
