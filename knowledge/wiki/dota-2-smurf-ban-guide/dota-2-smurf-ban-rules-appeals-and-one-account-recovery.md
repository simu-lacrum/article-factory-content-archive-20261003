**A Dota 2 smurf ban is not fixed by buying another account.** Valve has said that smurfing is not welcome, that alternate accounts can be linked to a player's main account, and that related accounts may receive penalties. The safest response is to stop using an alternate account for matchmaking manipulation, secure the account you own, and return to normal play.

This guide explains the policy boundary and the actions you can verify. It does not provide a way to hide account links, imitate a beginner, or evade enforcement.
