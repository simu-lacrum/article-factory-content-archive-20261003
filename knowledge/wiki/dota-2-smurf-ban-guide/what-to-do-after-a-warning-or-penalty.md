## What to do after a warning or penalty

1.   **Stop alternate-account matchmaking.** Do not queue a second profile while deciding what to do.
2.   **Secure the account you own.** Change the Steam password from a trusted device, confirm the recovery email, and review Steam Guard and authorised sessions.
3.   **Read the exact notice.** Save the visible message, affected account, game, and date. Do not infer a hidden reason from forum screenshots.
4.   **Use official support only.** If an appeal or ownership question is available, submit concise facts through Steam Support. Never send a password, mobile code, or recovery code to a third party.
5.   **Remove untrusted software.** Uninstall third-party loaders and review the security guidance in our [Dota 2 detection guide](https://dota2cheat.com/guides/dota-2-cheat-detection).

Support may not disclose detection details or reverse an enforcement action. A person promising a guaranteed unban, “clean flag,” or hardware reset is selling a claim, not access to Valve's internal records.
