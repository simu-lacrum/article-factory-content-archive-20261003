## Why a purchased account makes the problem worse

An account bought from a marketplace can have an unknown ownership history, previous reports, payment disputes, or recovery claims from its original owner. You cannot audit every login, match, or device associated with it. Sharing credentials with the seller also creates a separate security risk.

Do not follow advice about changing hardware, rotating IP addresses, losing early games, or keeping a particular percentage of wins. Those tactics are speculative evasion and may connect more accounts to the same pattern. They also leave you without a reliable account-recovery story if Steam Support asks for ownership evidence.
