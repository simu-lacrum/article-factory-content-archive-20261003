## Smurf status and behavior score are different

A behavior score reflects conduct signals such as communication and reports. Smurf enforcement concerns account and matchmaking misuse. Improving communication cannot erase an account-link decision, and changing accounts cannot repair a conduct history. Use the [behavior-score recovery plan](https://dota2cheat.com/guides/dota-2-behavior-score) for the conduct side of the problem.
