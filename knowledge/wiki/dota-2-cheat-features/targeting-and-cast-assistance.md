## Targeting and cast assistance

Targeting aids can sort nearby units, suggest a cast position, or choose a configured priority. Prediction may account for movement and projectile travel. Latency, immunity, dispels, reflection and vision still affect the result. A valid target is not always the correct target, so automation cannot replace the player’s decision.

Ask whether a module sends an input or only displays a suggestion. Ask what happens when two targets meet the same condition, and what stops the sequence if the ability is on cooldown. A short answer that names edge cases is stronger evidence than a longer feature list.
