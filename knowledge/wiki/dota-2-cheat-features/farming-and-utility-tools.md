## Farming and utility tools

Last-hit indicators, stack timers, rune reminders, camera controls and inventory panels may reduce repeated interface work. Their usefulness depends on role: a support may care about ward and enemy-item context, while a carry may value farming cues. Count observable jobs, not toggles. Five settings that change one panel are not five independent systems.

![Image 4: Dota 2 utility controls for creep interactions](https://dota2cheat.com/assets/dota2/dota2-440332b3deb42e5c7f9e8dfc.webp)

Utility claims should identify the trigger and the input they affect.
