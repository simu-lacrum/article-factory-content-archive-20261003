## Visual and information modules

Visual modules may draw ranges, cooldowns, inventory panels, ward reminders or entity markers. First ask whether the source is already visible to the player. A timer based on a visible event is different from a feature claiming to reveal data hidden by fog of war. The latter has a more direct enforcement precedent and should never be described as a harmless overlay.

For every indicator, check its trigger, duration, scope and fallback. Does it cover every hero or only a documented set? Does it show an estimate as an estimate? Can it be disabled without closing the rest of the client? Those details are more useful than “full vision” or “advanced ESP” as a headline. Our [Dota 2 maphack claims guide](https://dota2cheat.com/guides/dota-2-maphack-guide) explains why hidden-information wording deserves extra scrutiny.
