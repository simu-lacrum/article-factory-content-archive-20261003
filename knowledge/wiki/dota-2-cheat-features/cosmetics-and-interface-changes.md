## Cosmetics and interface changes

Cosmetic labels can refer to local visual changes, custom models or a skin changer. The label does not establish whether the change is visible only to the user, supported after updates, or permitted by the game’s rules. Treat a screenshot as evidence of appearance at one moment, not evidence of current operation or current support.
