## Conclusion

The choice of software depends on your goals in Deadlock. If you need a budget option for a couple of evenings, **Predator** or **Octarine** will suit you.

However, if you value your account’s security, want to easily win micro-duels for souls, and dominate the map, the choice narrows down to the two leaders:

*   **Melonity** is perfect for those who want to play using **Chams** and automated casting (**Hero Combos**).
*   **Cluster** is the undisputed **Top 1** for those who need the perfect **Aimbot** (for enemies, creeps, and souls), **Auto Parry**, flexible view settings (**FOV/World Color**), and the most reliable ban protection on the market.
