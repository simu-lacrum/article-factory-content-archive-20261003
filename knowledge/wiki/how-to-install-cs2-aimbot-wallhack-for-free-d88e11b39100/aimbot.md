### Aimbot

Aiming in a cluster is an art form in itself; the settings let you tailor it to your preferences and playstyle, and it won’t trigger anti-cheat thanks to built-in VAC protection. However, don’t set the aiming sensitivity too high, and make sure to set a slight delay before firing.

Let me explain in a little more detail.

*   FOV refers to the aimbot’s operational area; in other words, when an opponent enters this area, the aimbot will lock onto them.
*   Smooth controls how smoothly the aimbot locks onto targets.
*   Target priority determines which target is given priority if there are multiple targets within the FOV at once.
*   Hitboxes include areas such as the head, neck, spine, hips, arms, and legs — these are the areas the aimbot will target. Don’t set it to target only the head, to avoid raising unnecessary suspicion.

You can also customize it for each weapon type and adjust other minor settings to make your gameplay more inconspicuous.
