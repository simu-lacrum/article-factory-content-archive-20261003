### TriggerBot

This is a feature that automatically fires as soon as the aim is locked onto an enemy.

Yes, the aimbot just locks onto the target, while the triggerbot is responsible for actually firing the shot.

Here, you can adjust the delay between shots, the response time, and so on. I recommend exploring it yourself to gain a deeper understanding — it’s really simple and quick.
