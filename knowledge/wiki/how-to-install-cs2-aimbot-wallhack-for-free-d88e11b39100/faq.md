**2. How can I install Cluster?**

Create an account on the official Cluster website, download the launcher, sign in, and follow the instructions provided on the product page.

**3. Is there a free trial?**

New users may receive a seven-day trial after registering through an eligible OAuth method.

**4. Can Cluster settings be customized?**

Yes. You can adjust FOV, aim smoothing, target priority, hitboxes, visuals, delays, and weapon-specific configurations.

Cluster has a built-in, extremely powerful VAC protection module, as well as a Humanizer that makes the bot’s behavior appear more human-like. In all the time it has been in operation, not a single user has been banned.
