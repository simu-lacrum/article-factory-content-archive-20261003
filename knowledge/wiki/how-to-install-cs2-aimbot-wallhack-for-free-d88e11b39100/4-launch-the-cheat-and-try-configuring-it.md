### 4. Launch the cheat and try configuring it

> Important! Disable your antivirus before launching the client. It will prevent you from downloading the asset files needed for the wallhack and aimbot to work. These assets are simply a set of menu images that allow you to interact with the features. This happens for the same reasons: not all antivirus programs scan the files that the app downloads from servers, so by default they flag them as dangerous; however, some antivirus programs do scan them, and in that case, you won’t encounter any problems.

Once you’ve launched the cheat, don’t jump into a ranked match right away — take some time to familiarize yourself with its features.

To open the menu, press the “Insert” key

Start a match against bots and just see how the aimbot, wallhack, and other features work. Cluster comes with a pre-made config with out-of-the-box settings, so you can simply enable it in the config tab and try playing.

Customize the bindings and visuals to suit your preferences, but try not to go overboard.
