## What are DOTA 2 cheats?

Dota 2 cheats cover several different kinds of unfair assistance: scripts can automate decisions or input sequences, overlays can reorganize information, and map-related tools may claim to reveal or infer activity that the normal interface does not provide. The word “script” is not a safety category. A macro that repeats inputs, a rule-based automation, and an internal tool can behave differently while still creating an unfair advantage. Because Dota 2 changes through gameplay patches, a useful guide must state when it was reviewed and which mechanic or feature it describes. Readers should also distinguish public information from hidden or predicted information: a timer based on a visible event is not the same as direct access to unavailable state. CheatsGaming groups these topics by what the feature changes, not by whatever label makes it sound harmless.

DOTA 2 cheat terms at a glance
| Term | What it usually means | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scripts / combos | Automates an input sequence, timing window, or conditional action. | Which decisions are automated and whether patch timing has changed. |
| Overlays | Reformats cooldown, range, status, or reminder information. | Whether the underlying information is public, inferred, or hidden. |
| Map information | Claims to reveal or predict wards, movement, or hidden activity. | Direct observation versus inference and the known error rate. |
| Macros | Repeats or sequences inputs without necessarily reading game state. | Input method, rule context, and what advantage it creates. |
