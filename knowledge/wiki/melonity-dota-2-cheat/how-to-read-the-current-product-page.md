## How to read the current product page

1.   Check the status date and the Dota build named.
2.   Open the feature list and note hero, mode and utility exclusions.
3.   Read activation, downtime, refund and removal terms.
4.   Confirm the support route and what information it requests.
5.   Decide whether the platform terms fit the account you intend to use before payment.
