## What the interface suggests

The preserved screens show a platform organized around hero-specific automation, visual panels and general utilities. A category name is not a guarantee of scope. Before relying on a module, check the supported hero, mode, trigger, stop condition and date of the last build check.

Read “combo” as a sequence of configured rules, not as strategic judgment. Immunity, dispels, reflection, cooldowns and target priority can change whether a sequence is appropriate. Enable one module at a time and keep a note of the hero and build used for the test.
