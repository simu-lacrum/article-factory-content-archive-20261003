## Which profile fits which buyer?

A buyer who needs broad, per-hero configuration may prefer the product with current evidence for that scope. A buyer who values a smaller setup may prefer the listing with fewer, clearer modules. That is a conditional fit, not a claim that one platform always wins.

Score each product from zero to two for current status, feature clarity, setup requirements, plan transparency and support. Leave a field blank when the listing is silent. The higher score is only as reliable as the evidence behind it. Repeat the worksheet after a major patch.
