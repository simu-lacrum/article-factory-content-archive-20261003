### 4. nixware

This is a fairly old cheat that also offers 3 free days with a purchased subscription, but I ranked it 4th because some users get banned for using it — its VAC protection is questionable.

I also don’t like the design — it’s absolutely awful everywhere. I want to note that I haven’t really had any issues with the cheat working, but I’ve heard a lot of complaints from my friends and acquaintances.
