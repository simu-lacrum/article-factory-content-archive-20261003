### 2. xone

This cheat**doesn’t offer a free trial period, but they do give you a few free days added to your subscription with your first purchase** — just like everyone else, really. Except cluster.center

I ranked it second because the overall design isn’t bad (at least it’s easy to understand) and there are several versions of the cheat available, but I’m not too fond of the quality of the features.

**Let’s look at the pros:**

1.   Design. It’s just there, let’s put it that way. I’m completely satisfied with the cheat menu; the website is a bit lackluster, of course, but it’s generally acceptable.
2.   Technical support. They always respond; I haven’t had any major issues interacting with them.
3.   The core functionality works correctly; I do have some concerns about stability, but that’s hard to achieve.

Otherwise, I’d say that overall the project is fairly mediocre, but they’ve been on the market for a long time, which is why I decided to put them in second place.
