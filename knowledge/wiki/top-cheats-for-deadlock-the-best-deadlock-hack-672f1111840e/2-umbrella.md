## 2. Umbrella

It’s a pretty expensive cheat that, in my opinion, doesn’t quite justify the price tag given its functionality.

The monthly subscription costs $20, which automatically makes it the most expensive option on the list; however, it’s important to note that it offers some automation features and slightly more advanced visual settings. That said, the price difference between the cluster and this cheat is a full $16.

![Image 2: Top Cheats for Deadlock. The Best Deadlock Hack — image 2](https://cheatsgaming.com/media/medium/c67c99238ac148edb99cdbdf.jpg)
Let’s briefly go over the pros:

1.   Extensive features. Despite the price, there’s still a wide range of automation features
2.   Visuals — there are quite extensive customization options for visuals, such as the ability to change the color of the sky, but, to be honest, this only gets in the way of gameplay
3.   Design — the interfaces aren’t bad; trust me, there will be projects at the top where they paid absolutely no attention to this
