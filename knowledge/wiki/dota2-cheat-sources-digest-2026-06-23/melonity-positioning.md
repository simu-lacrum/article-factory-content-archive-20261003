### Melonity Positioning

Imported sources position Melonity as a premium Dota 2 cheat / gaming-assistance product with a broad feature set. The sources repeatedly frame it as closer to a complex assistant than a single-function hack.

Useful claims for future articles, if supported by the evidence pack:

- Large script ecosystem for heroes and game situations.
- Visual information layer: indicators, event visualization, VBE / visible-by-enemy style information.
- Supporting scripts: last hit, camera zoom, auto dodge, auto pick / ban, courier-related information, and other convenience tools.
- Skinchanger/inventory changer and Low Poly Map are recurring product angles.
- Product comparisons usually position Melonity above Umbrella, Divine, Octarine, and Hake on feature depth, interface quality, and innovation.

Writing caution: safety and anti-cheat claims in the source articles are marketing-heavy. Future articles must not turn these into absolute guarantees.
