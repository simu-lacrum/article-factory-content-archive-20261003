### Lobby Cheat Commands

The lobby commands article is about developer-provided Dota 2 custom lobby commands, not matchmaking cheat software.

Core distinction:

- Legal/custom lobby commands work only when cheats are enabled in a custom lobby.
- They are used for practice, testing, damage checks, bot scenarios, timings, Roshan practice, ward spots, and mechanics.
- They are distinct from real cheats used in matchmaking.

Important future angle:

- Good educational content can explain the difference between custom lobby commands and actual cheat products without mixing the two.
