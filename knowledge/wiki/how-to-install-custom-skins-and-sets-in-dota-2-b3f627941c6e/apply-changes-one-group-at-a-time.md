## Apply changes one group at a time

Keep the first test small: one hero or one cosmetic group. Start a non-competitive local environment, check models and effects, then close the game and inspect for errors. Installing dozens of replacements at once makes a broken asset difficult to isolate.

When the client updates, remove or pause unofficial replacements until compatibility is rechecked. For broader selection criteria, use our [Dota 2 cosmetic tool selection framework](https://cheatsgaming.com/games/dota-2/top-skinchangers-for-dota-2-best-skinchanger-373b10b67f31).
