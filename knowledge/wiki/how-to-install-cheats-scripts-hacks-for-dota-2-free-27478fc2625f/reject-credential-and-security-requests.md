## Reject credential and security requests

No Dota utility needs your Steam password, email code, recovery code or browser session cookie. Do not paste those values into a loader. Be suspicious of instructions to disable antivirus, firewall, Secure Boot or account protection broadly and permanently.

[Steam's account security recommendations](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/6639-EB3C-EC79-FF60) should be the baseline for protecting credentials, regardless of what a download page says.
