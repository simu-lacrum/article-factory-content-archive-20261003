**Free Dota 2 scripts** still carry technical and account trade-offs. A download can be a text configuration, a Workshop bot package, a macro profile or a closed executable; those are different artifacts with different risks. Identify exactly what you received before opening it, and do not treat “free” as evidence that a file is trustworthy.

For the current partner flow, open [melonity.gg/en](https://dota2cheat.com/go/melonity) in a new tab. Create an account there and read the current product, Windows and update notes before deciding whether the trade-offs fit your account.
