### Aimbot and Soul Aimbot

You can’t do without it. The aimbot in Cluster is, by far, the best I’ve ever seen. It has very flexible settings, letting you choose the crosshair, miss rate, and so on.

There’s also a soul aimbot here that lets you collect souls and deny them from your opponent with a single click, which gives you a huge advantage at the start of the game.
