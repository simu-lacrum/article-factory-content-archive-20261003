### Auto-Parry and Anti-Parry

I’m sure you’re already well aware of what auto-parry is: a feature that automatically blocks an opponent’s melee attack. I demonstrated it in the video above.

However, you might not know what anti-parry is. You can probably guess, but I’ll explain how it works. Anti-Parry automatically moves the camera away so you don’t get caught in the enemy’s parry and become vulnerable. That’s it — it’s an extremely useful feature that’s just as helpful as auto-parry.
