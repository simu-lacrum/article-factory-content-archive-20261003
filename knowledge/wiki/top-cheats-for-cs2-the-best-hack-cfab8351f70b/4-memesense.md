## 4. Memesense

A Semirage cheat that has proven itself since the release of CS2, as the developers quickly provided workarounds for server checks and VACLive.

If you’re not aware, Semirage is a failed attempt to combine legit and rage. The result is something that isn’t as safe as a full-fledged legit, but also not as interesting as rage.

**Let’s briefly go over the pros:**

1.   For the price, they offer a pretty decent number of feature
2.   Stable performance across different systems

As for the cons, I’ll highlight the absolutely awful and clunky design of everything, as well as its questionable security against VAC. Over the past year, there have been no complaints from users about VAC bans, but knowing what the product looks like from the inside — this is unlikely to last forever.
