## 3. Fatality

Another fairly old and well-known HvH cheat, aimed primarily at players who prefer to rage. I also don’t recommend it for people who want to stay off VAC’s radar.

Let’s briefly go over the pros:

1.   Fatality frequently delights its users with events like a wheel of fortune or Easter egg hunts right in the software menu
2.   Ability to load custom skins
3.   Affordable price compared to its direct competitor — Neverlose

However, the interface is completely clunky, and the stability of their driver is questionable: BSODs can occur on some systems.

Let me emphasize this again:

> Do not use Rage HvH cheats if you care about your gaming account. Security in CS2 can only be provided by external legit solutions, such as cluster.center or something similar.
