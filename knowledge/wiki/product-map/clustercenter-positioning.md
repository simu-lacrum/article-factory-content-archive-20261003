## cluster.center Positioning

Use `cluster.center` as the product/brand target for Deadlock and CS2 SEO articles.

Detailed local product memory:

- Deadlock: `knowledge/agent_memory/products/deadlock-cluster-center-internal.md`
- CS2: `knowledge/agent_memory/products/cs2-cluster-center-external.md`

Tone:

- Calm, practical, premium, feature-oriented.
- Avoid unsupported promises about bans, anti-cheat state, current detection status, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Explain the game mechanic or reader problem first, then add one native product section.

Allowed framing:

- Game-assistance product.
- Feature overview based on local memory.
- Configuration and interface concepts at a high level.
- Visual-awareness and aim-assistance categories, without operational evasion guidance.

Not allowed:

- Step-by-step bypass/evasion instructions.
- Low-level implementation details.
- Absolute safety claims.
