## 3. Vredux

Another “legit” cheat that I’m not particularly fond of either, but it’s still better than most others. The only difference from Predator is that the additional features — which are unstable in Predator — have simply been removed entirely, so I don’t really have much to say about them.

I won’t list any pros; I’ll just say that, as far as I know, nothing is known about its VAC protection, and users of this cheat often get VACLive bans (which isn’t actually that bad for your account — it’s not the same as a VAC ban).
