## Why hidden client data is a detection surface

[Valve's official Dota 2 honeypot account](https://www.dota2.com/newsentry/3677788723152833273) describes a ban wave aimed at software that read internal client information unavailable during normal play. Valve created a hidden area specifically to identify that behavior.

The post does not enumerate modern maphack methods, and it should not be reverse-engineered into evasion advice. It establishes that hidden-data access can itself become evidence.
