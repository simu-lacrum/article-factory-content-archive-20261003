## What the label can include

Some products draw unit positions or ward locations. Others watch for effects that the client already receives and convert them into a minimap alert. A third group estimates movement from last-seen position. These methods can look similar in a screenshot while relying on different information.

Ask whether a marker is exact, delayed, estimated or triggered by a visible clue. Without that answer, “full map” is marketing language rather than a specification.
