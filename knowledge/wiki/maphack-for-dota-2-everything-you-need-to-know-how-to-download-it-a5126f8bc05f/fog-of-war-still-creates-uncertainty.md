## Fog of war still creates uncertainty

Even extra information does not show player intent, future pathing, smoke coordination or whether a visible event is bait. Stale positions can be worse than no overlay because they create false confidence. Illusions, summons and rapid movement add more ambiguity.

Map information also does not replace ward placement, timing knowledge or team communication. A user can still misread the fight while looking at more markers.
