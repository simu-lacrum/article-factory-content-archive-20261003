## How to assess a maphack listing

*   Separate exact positions from predictions and alerts.
*   Check the status date against the current Dota build.
*   Look for stated exclusions and known false positives.
*   Reject “VAC-proof” or permanent-undetected claims.
*   Verify delivery, activation, downtime and support terms.
