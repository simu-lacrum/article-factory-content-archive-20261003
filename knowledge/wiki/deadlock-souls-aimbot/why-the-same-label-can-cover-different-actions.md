## Why the same label can cover different actions

Cheat interfaces are not built around a shared naming standard. “Soul Aimbot” may describe a complete sequence that aims and fires, or it may describe only the firing step. “Soul Stealer” can be another name for the same combined workflow. This is not a technical contradiction; it is inconsistent product language.

Read the verbs in the description. Phrases such as _aims at souls_, _locks onto orbs_ or _moves to the soul_ indicate aim movement. Phrases such as _shoots automatically_, _fires on the orb_ or _secures the soul_ indicate a trigger action. If both appear, the feature likely combines both stages.

Settings provide a second check. FOV and smoothing belong to aim movement; delay and fire conditions belong to trigger behaviour. A hotkey can control either one, so the key binding by itself does not identify the function.
