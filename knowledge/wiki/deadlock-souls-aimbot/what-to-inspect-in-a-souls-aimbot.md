## What to inspect in a souls aimbot

The feature name alone is not enough. Look for controls that explain how the aimbot selects and follows an orb:

*   **Orb targeting:** an explicit soul, trooper soul or deny-soul target type.
*   **Field of view:** the area in which the feature is allowed to acquire an orb.
*   **Aim speed or smoothing:** how quickly the crosshair moves onto the orb.
*   **Activation:** always-on behaviour, a hold key or a toggle.
*   **Priority:** whether enemy denies, friendly confirms or the nearest orb are handled first.

A generic player aimbot is not automatically a souls aimbot. The menu or documentation needs to show that soul orbs are part of the target list. Without that evidence, a regular aim demo only proves that the product can move the crosshair toward heroes.
