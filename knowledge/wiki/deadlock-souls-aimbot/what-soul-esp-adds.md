## What soul ESP adds

Soul ESP is the visual layer. It can highlight friendly and enemy orbs, draw a marker through effects or distance, and help distinguish an available soul from the rest of the fight. It does not need to move the crosshair or shoot.

![Image 3: Cluster Deadlock ESP settings with object preview and visual controls](https://cheatsgaming.com/images/articles/deadlock-souls-aimbot/cluster-soul-esp-settings.webp)

The ESP menu controls what is drawn on screen; it is separate from the aimbot's target movement.

When comparing screenshots, check whether the menu names soul orbs directly. A general object ESP may support many entities, while a soul-specific option makes the intended use clearer.

![Image 4: Deadlock gameplay preview with Cluster ESP target markers and menu](https://cheatsgaming.com/images/articles/deadlock-souls-aimbot/cluster-soul-esp-preview.webp)

A gameplay preview is most useful when it can be matched to named controls in the same product interface.
