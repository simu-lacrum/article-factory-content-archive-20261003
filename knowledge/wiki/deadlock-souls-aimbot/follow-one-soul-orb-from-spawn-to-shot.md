## Follow one soul orb from spawn to shot

When a trooper dies, its soul orb rises for a short contest window. Following that single orb makes the feature roles clear:

1.   **Soul ESP** marks the orb or adds information around it.
2.   **Souls aimbot** moves the crosshair toward the orb according to its FOV, speed or smoothing settings.
3.   **Soul triggerbot** fires when the orb is inside the configured crosshair condition.

A combined system can perform steps two and three in quick succession. In a short clip that may look like one action, but the settings screen should reveal whether aim movement and firing can be configured independently.
