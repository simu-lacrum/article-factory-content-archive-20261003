## Valve has documented hidden-data detection

In [Valve's 40,000-account Dota enforcement report](https://www.dota2.com/newsentry/3677788723152833273), Valve explained that it added a data area ordinary clients would never read. Accounts using software that accessed the area were identified and permanently banned.

This example does not reveal the current system, but it disproves the idea that a working hidden-information feature is automatically invisible to the developer.
