## 3. Xone

Xone is quite popular, but it’s infamous for having been detected, and all its users were banned by VAC due to a very silly mistake by the developers; they never really recovered from that. Overall, I don’t have much to say about this cheat, except that it has a pretty decent design compared to Distort, for example, but in my opinion, Cluster looks better in terms of quality and value.

However, let’s briefly go over the pros:

1.   Decent design
2.   The basic set of features generally works reliably

But that’s about it — their website isn’t very user-friendly, but otherwise everything is fine
