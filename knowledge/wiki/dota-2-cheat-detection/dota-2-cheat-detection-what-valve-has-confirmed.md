**Dota 2 cheat detection cannot be reduced to a public “detected” list.** Valve can change its client, server signals, review process, and enforcement timing. A tool that launches without a visible penalty has only avoided a visible penalty during that window; it has not earned a permanent safety guarantee.

The useful question is what Valve has actually published, what a seller merely claims, and what remains intentionally undisclosed.
