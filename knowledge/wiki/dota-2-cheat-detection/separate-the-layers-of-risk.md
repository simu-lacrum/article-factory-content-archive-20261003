## Separate the layers of risk

*   **Account risk:** a VAC or game penalty can be permanent.
*   **Security risk:** an unknown loader may request credentials, administrator access, or a driver.
*   **Technical risk:** a Dota update can break a dependency or leave stale components behind.
*   **Evidence risk:** a seller may not publish dates, supported builds, or a removal path.

Check the current Dota update history against any product status date. If a tool requires Steam credentials, browser cookies, or an unexplained global security exception, stop. Do not trade account access for a feature list.
