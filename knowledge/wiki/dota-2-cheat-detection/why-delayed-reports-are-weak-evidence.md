## Why delayed reports are weak evidence

Public ban reports are incomplete. A player may not identify the software, may post months later, or may never post. Sellers can remove negative comments, and a small user base produces too little data for a reliable rate. Enforcement can also be delayed, so a short test cannot establish a long-term outcome.

![Image 4: Dota 2 cheat status and delayed enforcement example](https://dota2cheat.com/assets/dota2/dota2-eb95599fc32cdb9037b264a8.webp)

Use a simple evidence ladder: an official Valve statement is stronger than a vendor badge; a dated, reproducible observation is stronger than an anonymous screenshot; and the absence of a report is not evidence of absence.
