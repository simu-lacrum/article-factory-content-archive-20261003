## A responsible conclusion

Read the official policy first, date every product claim, and assume that a third-party advantage can carry permanent account consequences. Do not publish or follow anti-cheat bypass instructions. If you need to learn a mechanic, use a private lobby or the supported Workshop tools instead.

Found an outdated claim or broken source? [Send a correction request](https://dota2cheat.com/corrections).
