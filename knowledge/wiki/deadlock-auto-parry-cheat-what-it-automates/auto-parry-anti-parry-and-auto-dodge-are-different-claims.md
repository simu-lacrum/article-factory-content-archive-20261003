## Auto parry, anti parry and auto dodge are different claims

These labels can sit next to each other in a menu, but they describe different jobs:

*   **Auto parry:** automating the defender's parry timing.
*   **Anti parry:** an advertised adjustment to the attacker's timing or attack sequence.
*   **Auto dodge:** an advertised dodge response, which should not be treated as another name for parrying.

For example, [Cluster's Deadlock feature descriptions](https://cluster.center/en/deadlock) separate Auto Parry, Anti Parry and Dodger. Its Auto Parry entry refers to supported melee interactions and explicitly notes that latency and game updates can affect results.

Coverage also needs a closer look. Public feature lists may name several melee-related interactions under Auto Parry, including Guardian attacks and Viscous's Puddle Punch. Those are a provider's coverage claims. They shouldn't be transferred to another product just because its menu uses the same heading.
