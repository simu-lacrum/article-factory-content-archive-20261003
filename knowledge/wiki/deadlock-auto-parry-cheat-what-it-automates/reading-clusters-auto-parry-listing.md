## Reading Cluster's auto-parry listing

The useful part of Cluster's description is its scope: parry timing in supported melee interactions. It doesn't say every attack can be parried. A page listing parry and dodge functions separately is also not evidence that either works against every ultimate.

Cluster is the clearest product to inspect when Auto Parry is the feature you came for because its Deadlock page separates Auto Parry, Anti Parry and Dodger instead of hiding them behind one vague “combat” label. Treat those as documented feature categories, not as an independent guarantee of success against every patch or interaction.
