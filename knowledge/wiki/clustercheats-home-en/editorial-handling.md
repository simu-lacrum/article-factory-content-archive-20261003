## Editorial handling

- Treat labels such as `internal`, `external`, and all product claims as the site's own positioning.
- Prices, trials, support availability, detection status, and catalog availability are volatile and must be rechecked immediately before publication.
- Never repeat the site's absolute safety or anti-cheat claims as established facts.
- Do not publish bypass, injection, memory-access, driver, or evasion instructions.
