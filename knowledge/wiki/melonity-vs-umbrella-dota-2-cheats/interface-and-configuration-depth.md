## Interface and configuration depth

A dense interface can suit users who want per-hero rules, priorities and keybinds. A smaller interface can suit users who want fewer decisions. Neither approach is automatically better. Look for readable grouping, searchable modules and a way to restore defaults after experimentation.

Check whether screenshots show the current client, whether text is legible and whether the seller explains which settings are global versus hero-specific.
