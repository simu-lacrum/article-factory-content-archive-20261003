## Access, delivery and support

Judge the actual plan you can buy: duration, currency, activation flow and what happens during downtime. Automatic key delivery is useful, but a product still needs an activation URL and a support route for rejected or already-used keys.

Before paying, read the refund terms and determine whether time continues to run while a Dota update is being tested. If the listing does not answer that question, ask support before checkout.
