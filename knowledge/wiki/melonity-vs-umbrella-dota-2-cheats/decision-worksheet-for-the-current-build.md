## Decision worksheet for the current build

Create two columns and write only facts visible on current listings: last test date, supported Windows versions, active hero modules, visual functions, plan duration, delivery method, activation URL and support channel. Mark any missing item as unknown. Do not fill a gap with a forum comment or an old promotional video.

Then assign three priorities you actually need. A larger product does not win by default if its extra modules are irrelevant to your heroes or role. The product with clearer current evidence may be the more practical choice even when its headline list is shorter.

Repeat the worksheet after a major Dota patch. The result can change without either brand becoming permanently “best,” which is why this article avoids an unqualified winner.

Keep support quality measurable. Record how clearly each product explains downtime, whether answers refer to the current build and whether a failed key has a documented resolution path. Fast replies are useful, but a fast unsupported claim is not better evidence than a slower answer tied to release notes.

Do not let a temporary discount decide the worksheet. A shorter plan with clear delivery and current evidence can be easier to evaluate than a long commitment made during an uncertain update window.
