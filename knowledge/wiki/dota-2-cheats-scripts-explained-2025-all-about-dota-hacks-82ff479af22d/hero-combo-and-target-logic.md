## Hero combo and target logic

Combo logic selects actions based on configuration and current conditions. It may choose a target, apply an item, cast abilities in order and stop when the target becomes invalid. Quality depends on how many exceptions the author has modeled.

No combo script understands strategy by default. It may execute quickly while ignoring buyback, enemy reinforcements, spell reflection, dispels or the need to save one ability. Automation should be described as a rule system, not intelligence.
