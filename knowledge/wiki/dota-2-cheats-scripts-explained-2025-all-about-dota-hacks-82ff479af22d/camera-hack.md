### Camera Hack

One of the most difficult to implement and useful scripts in the cheat. With it, you can zoom in and out on the map with the mouse wheel, as if you were playing Starcraft. To be honest, Dota without this script seems extremely inconvenient, and you will do everything you can to get back to playing with it.
