### Invoker

Invoker, one of the most mechanically complex heroes in Dota 2, is simplified thanks to autocast scripts. They automate the casting and application of spell combinations: Sun Strike in conjunction with Chaos Meteor, quickly switching between Quas, Wex, and Exort. Melonity’s version allows you to bind complex combinations to a single key, making it possible for beginners to perform professional-level actions without having to memorize orb rotations.
