### Arc-Warden

For Arc Warden, the scripts in the cheats brilliantly handle controlling his clones and spamming Sparks. The Spark Wraith spam function automatically releases these charges in the right places, attacking enemies.

In addition, the script controls clones for farming: the user can set up clones to automatically farm creeps on the line or in the forest, directing them along efficient routes while the main hero is busy with other tasks.

> Well, as you can imagine, scripts, for example, on Pudge, automate the hook, on Shadow Find they press coils, the script for Enigma helps in using Black Hole, taking into account the position of opponents and the readiness of allies, and also uses it in combination with Blink Dagger.
