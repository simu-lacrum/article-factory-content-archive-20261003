### • Scripts for heroes

Scripts for heroes in cheats are one of the most important categories, aimed at automating complex abilities and combinations for characters. As you can imagine, such scripts turn the most gameplay-complex heroes into simple picks, and in the hands of professionals, into weapons of mass destruction.

Essentially, they automate procs, illusion control, and item synergy. All of this happens with perfect timing.

In order not to make the article too long, I will describe and demonstrate how such scripts work only on the two most popular heroes among cheaters — Invoker and Arc Warden.
