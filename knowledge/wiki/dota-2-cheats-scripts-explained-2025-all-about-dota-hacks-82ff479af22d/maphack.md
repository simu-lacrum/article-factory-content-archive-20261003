## MapHack

One of the fundamental functions of every hack, which reveals the enemy’s position in the fog of war. It can be classified as an auxiliary function, but I decided to highlight it because of its importance.

You may have already seen an example of maphack in action in one of the screenshots above.

![Image 26](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:302/1*LPZAaWrsrB_j-i9kvPMfMw.png)

Melonity.gg MapHack indicator

At the moment, it does not show the enemy’s position in the fog of war in real time, since Valve recently changed the way the fog of war works, which is why it became impossible to obtain the position using Dota 2 data.
