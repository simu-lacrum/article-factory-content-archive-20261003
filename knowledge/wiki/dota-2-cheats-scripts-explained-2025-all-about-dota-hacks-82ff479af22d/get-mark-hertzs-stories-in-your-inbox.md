**Skinchanger(inventory changer)**

In general, the skinchanger is a completely separate module, but I would still classify it as a visual element. I think you have a pretty good idea of what it does: it adds all skins, arcane items, landscapes, weather effects, and items directly to your inventory.

However, there are several options for its technical implementation. Quite recently, Melonity introduced an option that completely replicates the mechanics of applying Valve skins, which is why the FPS does not drop during gameplay.

However, that’s not all cheats are capable of. For example, you can play on Valve servers on a unique landscape — Low Poly or play with custom skins.
