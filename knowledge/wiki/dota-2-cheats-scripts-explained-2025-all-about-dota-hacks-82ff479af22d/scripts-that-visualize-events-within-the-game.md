These include scripts that visualize information coming from players on the map.

For example, displaying hidden abilities, the direction or position of their use, as well as the radius, in the case of AOE.

But that’s not all the information you’ll see about enemy heroes. As soon as the cheat sees that you can destroy an enemy hero with a single ability using your abilities and items, an indicator will appear above them.

![Image 11](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:528/1*FZJyUXENH7R_gL1JNWpgeQ.png)

This also includes scripts that display information directly about your hero. The most useful of all such functions is VBE, or Visible by Enemy.

This script uses a beautiful animation (specifically in Melonity) to signal that your character is within the field of vision of enemy heroes. 

It is worth noting that all visuals in this section can be customized and selected from the options provided.
