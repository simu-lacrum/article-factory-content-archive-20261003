### Auto Dodge

It is also one of the most useful scripts, which automatically dodges enemy abilities. For example, Hook of Pudge or various stuns.

When activated, it uses all possible items and abilities that allow the hero to quickly change their position, or takes control of the hero for a short period of time to avoid enemy abilities.
