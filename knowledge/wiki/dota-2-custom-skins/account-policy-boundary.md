## Account-policy boundary

Steam's official guidance warns that third-party modifications providing an advantage can be treated as cheats and advises care with custom skins and scripts. “Cosmetic,” “client-side” or “inventory-free” describes architecture, not future enforcement. There is no honest permanent-status promise.

Keep a simple change log with the package version, the cosmetic group you enabled and the date you restored it. This makes it possible to identify a patch-related failure without guessing which archive changed the client. If you cannot reproduce the change from your notes, the installation was not documented well enough.
