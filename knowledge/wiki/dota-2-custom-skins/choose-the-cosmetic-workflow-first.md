## Choose the cosmetic workflow first

Use Valve's official Dota 2 Workshop requirements when you are creating an asset for submission. Use supported game or marketplace flows for cosmetics that should belong to your inventory. If a tool promises a local replacement without ownership, treat it as an unofficial modification rather than an alternative Workshop install.

Local-only changes may be visible only on your client. They do not create a tradeable item and they do not prove that other players see the same model. A tutorial that calls a replacement an “unlock” is describing the result loosely.
