## Keep the workflow reversible

Save the package version and your restore notes, keep the normal game profile easy to select and remove unofficial files when access ends. A clean rollback is more valuable than a large cosmetic library you cannot explain. If a page cannot tell you what changes, how to undo them or which build it supports, do not treat the gallery as documentation.

Found an outdated claim or broken source? [Send a correction request](https://dota2cheat.com/corrections).
