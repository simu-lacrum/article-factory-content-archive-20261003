**Installing custom Dota 2 skins** starts with choosing a supported path. An official Workshop submission, an item already attached to your Steam inventory and a third-party local replacement may look similar in a screenshot, but they are delivered and governed differently. Decide which one you mean before downloading anything.

For the current partner flow, open [melonity.gg/en](https://dota2cheat.com/go/melonity) in a new tab. Create an account there and read the current product, Windows and update notes before deciding whether the trade-offs fit your account.
