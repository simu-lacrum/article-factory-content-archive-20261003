## Pause after a Dota update

Models, particles and file validation can change with a patch. If a replacement is not marked compatible with the current build, remove or pause it rather than layering another pack on top. Watch the official Dota 2 update history when checking whether the client changed after the package was published.

Missing models, broken effects or a crash are signals to restore the last known clean state. Do not keep launching the client to “see if it settles.” If the issue remains after documented removal, use Steam's official file-verification flow and contact the package publisher through the original channel.
