## What to do after a warning or penalty

1.   Stop using alternate accounts for lower-skill matchmaking.
2.   Secure the main Steam account and remove unknown sessions.
3.   Review account notifications and official support information.
4.   Do not pay anyone who claims they can remove an enforcement action.
5.   Play normally on one account and allow the system to collect legitimate match history.

A warning is not an invitation to rotate to another profile. Repeated evasion can connect more accounts to the same pattern.
