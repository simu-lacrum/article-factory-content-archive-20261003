## What Valve calls smurfing

Smurfing generally describes using an alternate account to play below a player's established skill or otherwise manipulate matchmaking. Shared household access, a genuinely new player and a returning user can look different from deliberate rank manipulation, but only Valve controls enforcement and account classification.

In [Smurfing is Not Welcome in Dota](https://www.dota2.com/newsentry/3692442542242977036), Valve announced action against 90,000 smurf accounts and said associated main accounts could receive penalties ranging from behavior-score adjustments to permanent bans.
