On Reddit, thematic forums, and simply in comments, reports of game bans from the developer began to appear more and more often. Moreover, it is not cheaters who complain about these bans, but ordinary hard workers. Some mistakenly perceive this ban as a VAC ban, but it has nothing to do with the latter.

There were several types of bans in Dota:

1.   Ban for very low integrity.
2.   Ban by patrol verdict (has not been in effect for several years, after the arc warden fix).
3.   Ban by the smurf system (peaked in popularity among esports players and streamers).

Ban for detecting cheat functions (there were only two major waves in the entire history of Dota). And at the moment, it is completely impossible to block individual cheat functions. If you want to learn more about this, I recommend checking out other articles on my profile or trying it out for yourself with the [melonity dota cheat.](https://melonity.gg/)

It is quite easy to distinguish between these bans. If your Steam profile says “1 game ban,” then you fall under one of the first three points. If it says “1 VAC ban,” then you have fallen under the cheating wave. Look for the mention of VAC in your Steam profile, not inside Dota.

After analyzing all the accounts provided by players on Reddit, as well as some of our users, it is easy to conclude that Valve has now begun to crack down on the smurf system. No one has studied all the algorithms behind how it works with 100% certainty. You can be banned on a new account because of an old IP address, a winning streak, aggressive play with difficult heroes, or even a random mention of your previously used data (email, phone number) on a new account, even if you unlinked them a long time ago. You can be banned at any time for anything. Some players play without a spoof, swap accounts, and are not banned for years. This is where Valve’s clownish random system comes into play.

Only one thing can be stated with certainty: certain types of accounts are blocked by the smurf system. The account has been linked at least once, has played less than 1,000 matches, has had several smurf matches, or was registered in 2022–2024. All such accounts are inherently unreliable and have a high chance of being banned quickly, even if you don’t use cheats, as they have nothing to do with it.
