## Why a purchased account is not a solution

An account sold as “aged,” “calibrated” or “safe” may be stolen, recovered by its original owner, linked to previous misconduct or already under review. The buyer cannot verify its complete history. Account trading also creates credential and payment disputes outside normal support.

Advice about ideal match counts, win rates or hardware fingerprints is speculative and encourages evasion. Valve does not publish a threshold that makes a smurf acceptable.
