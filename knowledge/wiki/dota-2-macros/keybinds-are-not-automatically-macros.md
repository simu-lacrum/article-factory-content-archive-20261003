## Keybinds are not automatically macros

A normal in-game keybind maps one supported action to a key. A macro may send several inputs, repeat an input or add precise delays. Document exactly what one activation does. A gaming keyboard or mouse does not make every stored sequence permitted in every platform or tournament environment.
