## Check policy before testing

Valve's VAC guidance makes clear that third-party modifications providing an advantage can lead to a ban. “Hardware macro” is not a universal exemption. Competition organizers may apply stricter rules, so read the current event rulebook and disable unapproved profiles before joining.
