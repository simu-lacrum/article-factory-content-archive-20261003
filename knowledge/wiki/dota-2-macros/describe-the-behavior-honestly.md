## Describe the behavior honestly

A macro page is useful when it tells readers exactly what is replayed, what can interrupt it and how to reset it. It should not turn a fixed sequence into a “smart” guarantee or imply that a device label removes account policy. Precise scope makes a decision possible; broad claims only hide the trade-off.
