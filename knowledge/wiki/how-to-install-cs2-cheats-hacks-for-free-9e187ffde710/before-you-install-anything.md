## Before you install anything

Do not rush the download.

Most bad installs start the same way: someone searches “free hack for CS2”, opens a reposted file, skips the official website, and then wonders why the setup feels suspicious.

Before installing any cheat for CS2, check these basics:

*   Are you on the official cluster.center website?
*   Is the product page specifically for CS2?
*   Are you logged into your own account?
*   Does your account actually have product access?
*   Are the instructions coming from the official source?
*   Are you avoiding random archives, mirrors, and third-party launchers?

This matters more than people think. With a CS2 cheat, the first mistake is usually not a setting. It is downloading from the wrong place.
