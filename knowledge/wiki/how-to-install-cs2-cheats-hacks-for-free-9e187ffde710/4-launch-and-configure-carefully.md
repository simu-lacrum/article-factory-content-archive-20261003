### 4. Launch and configure carefully

After setup, open the product according to the official instructions and configure it slowly.

The cluster includes a default CFG that is available to all users and works right out of the box. I recommend going to the training section and testing it with all its features. Read

Do not turn on every feature at once. A cheat for CS2 is easier to understand when you start with a few basic settings, test how they feel, and adjust from there.

For a first run, focus on comfort:

*   readable visuals;
*   simple keybinds;
*   modest aim settings;
*   no overloaded screen;
*   one weapon profile at a time.

That is enough to learn the menu without making your game look chaotic.
