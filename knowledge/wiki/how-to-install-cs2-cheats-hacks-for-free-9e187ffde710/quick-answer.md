## Quick answer

If you want to install a CS2 hack, the safe high-level flow is:

1.   Go to the official cluster.center website.
2.   Register an account or log in.
3.   Check that you have access to the CS2 External product.(**All users who register via OAuth receive a free 7-day subscription to all Cluster products and cheats.**)
4.   Download only from the official source.
5.   Follow the official instructions([**click**](https://telegra.ph/Instrukciya-po-ustanovke-Cluster-04-24)) from cluster.center.
6.   Launch and configure the cheat for CS2 carefully, using conservative settings first.

That is the whole idea. The details should come from the official website and support channels, not from random files, reposts, or “free CS2 hack” archives from search results.
