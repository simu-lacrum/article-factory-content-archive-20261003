### Can I install a CS2 hack for free?

Yes, clustercheats.com offers a free seven-day trial period for all new users, which is granted automatically.

However, be careful with cheats that are completely free — they can be dangerous for your account and contain malware. It’s better not to take the risk and just keep playing on cluster.
