## Policy and account risk

[Valve Anti-Cheat guidance for third-party advantages](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/571A-97DA-70E9-FF74) makes clear that third-party modifications providing an advantage can lead to a ban. The label “hardware macro” is not a universal exemption.

Competition organizers may also apply stricter rules than ordinary matchmaking. If an event matters, read its current rulebook and disable unapproved device profiles before joining.
