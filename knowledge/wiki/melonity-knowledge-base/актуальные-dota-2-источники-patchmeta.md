### Актуальные Dota 2 источники (patch/meta)

- [Dota 2 Patch Notes](https://www.dota2.com/patches) — официальный источник текущего patch number и patch notes
- [Dota 2 News](https://www.dota2.com/news) — официальный источник крупных update/newsentry
- [Dota2ProTracker Meta](https://dota2protracker.com/meta) — текущие high-MMR/pro hero trends, win rate, matches
- [Dota2ProTracker Tier Lists](https://dota2protracker.com/tier-lists) — role-based pub/pro tier lists
- [Dotabuff Hero Meta](https://www.dotabuff.com/heroes?view=meta) — массовая статистика героев/фасетов
- [Steam Support — Valve Anti-Cheat](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/571A-97DA-70E9-FF74) — базовый источник по VAC terminology
