## What are CS2 cheats?

CS2 cheats are unofficial tools or automations that change the information a player can see, the way aiming or firing behaves, or the inputs the game receives. ESP and wallhack labels usually refer to extra player information; aimbot and triggerbot labels describe aim or firing assistance; radar and DMA describe a display method or technical setup rather than a safety level. The useful questions are therefore specific: what feature is being discussed, which CS2 build was reviewed, what evidence supports the description, and what account or device risk remains? Valve states that third-party modifications designed to give one player an advantage are cheats or hacks, and that VAC bans are permanent. A product status badge cannot override that policy or prove future compatibility. This section links focused explanations instead of treating every CS2 cheat as the same product.

CS2 cheat terms at a glance
| Term | What it usually means | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ESP / wallhack | Adds player, health, distance, skeleton, or visibility information. | Which data is shown, whether it is current, and the exact review date. |
| Aimbot / triggerbot | Changes crosshair movement, target selection, or firing timing. | FOV, smoothing, visibility logic, limitations, and input behavior. |
| Radar | Moves position data into a map-like or relative view. | What positions are included and how stale or occluded data is handled. |
| Internal / external / DMA | Describes where components run or how data may be accessed. | Architecture is not proof of safety; check evidence, updates, and risk. |
