## Functional

*   ### Aimbot

Provides configurable aim assistance for supported targets. Available parameters should be listed directly on the page and shown in current screenshots.

*   ### TriggerBot

Automatically fires when the configured targeting conditions are met. Its behaviour depends on the selected settings and current product version.

*   ### ESP

Displays configurable information about supported game objects and opponents through an on-screen overlay.

*   ### HUD

Shows selected product and gameplay information in a compact interface. The exact available elements depend on the current version.
