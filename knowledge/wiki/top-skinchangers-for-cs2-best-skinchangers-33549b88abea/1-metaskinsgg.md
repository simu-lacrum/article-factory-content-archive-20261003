## 1. MetaSkins.gg

As far as I understand, MetaSkins is the Dota Changer that changed its name. I’ve been familiar with dota changer for a long time, the project has been around for at least 5 years, which automatically makes it the oldest and most reliable on the list. At the moment it is distributed completely free of charge and has in itself [both skinchanger for CS2](https://metaskins.gg/en) and for Dota.

Let’s briefly go over the pros:

1.   Ability to open cases/capsules and set the chance to drop rare items
2.   Available charms, skins and knives preview system right inside the changer menu
3.   As we’ll see next, having the menu itself is a plus in itself
4.   Ability to apply agent patches, keychains, graffiti and stickers just like in the game itself
5.   Ability to add and use sets of music
6.   Work experience
7.   Availability of dota changer right in the launcher
