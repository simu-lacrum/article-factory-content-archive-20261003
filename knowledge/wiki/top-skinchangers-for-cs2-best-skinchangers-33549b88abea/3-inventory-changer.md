## 3. Inventory Changer

A newer project that is superior to №2 in terms of functionality, however I don’t trust it as it has been around for less than a year.

Briefly on the pros:

1.   In-game menu
2.   Ability to apply key rings, stickers and patches
3.   Profile changer that allows you to change your rank, likes and rating, usually only the ability to add medals is available. This is only seen by people in the same lobby as you when searching for the game
4.   Music kits
