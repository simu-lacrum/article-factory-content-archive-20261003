**Dota 2 skin changers** are local cosmetic tools, not a type of item in your Steam inventory. Start by deciding whether you want an official Workshop project, an owned in-game cosmetic, or an unofficial program that changes what your client renders. That distinction determines what the files do, who can see the result and which account-policy warnings apply.

For the current partner flow, open [melonity.gg/en](https://dota2cheat.com/go/melonity) in a new tab. Create an account there and read the current product, Windows and update notes before deciding whether the trade-offs fit your account.
