## A short comparison checklist

*   Does the page identify local-only visibility?
*   Are supported cosmetic categories and exclusions specific?
*   Is the last compatibility check dated to a Dota build?
*   Are credentials, permissions and changed files explained?
*   Can you restore the original state without a private support chat?
*   Does the seller avoid permanent or “undetected” promises?

The best choice is the one you can describe precisely: what is changed, where it runs, what happens after a patch and how you undo it. If a listing cannot answer those questions, a polished gallery is not enough evidence.
