## Three cosmetic paths are not interchangeable

Valve's supported route is the Workshop. Its Dota 2 Workshop requirements describe how creators prepare and submit assets. An approved item is not the same thing as a local replacement downloaded from a third-party site. Inventory cosmetics are another category: they are attached to an account through supported game or marketplace systems.

A skin changer usually changes local presentation. If another player cannot see the replacement, it is a client-side visual change rather than a newly owned item. A responsible listing says this plainly and does not call a local preview an inventory unlock.
