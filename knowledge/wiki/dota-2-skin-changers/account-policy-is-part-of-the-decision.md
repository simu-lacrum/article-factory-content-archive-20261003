## Account policy is part of the decision

Steam's official VAC guidance warns that third-party modifications providing an advantage can be treated as cheats. “Cosmetic only,” “client-side” or “VAC-proof” wording cannot turn that warning into a guarantee. Keep valuable accounts and important events out of experiments whose status you cannot verify.
