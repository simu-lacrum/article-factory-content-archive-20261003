## 2. Umbrella

Umbrella is also not a newcomer, having been on the market for about nine years. The product is slightly more affordable in terms of price, literally a couple of dollars cheaper than Melonity, but the quality is also slightly lower. The main difference is in the humanizer; the one mentioned above from Melonity is the best on the market. And although users are not currently receiving vac-live bans, everything may change in the near future.
