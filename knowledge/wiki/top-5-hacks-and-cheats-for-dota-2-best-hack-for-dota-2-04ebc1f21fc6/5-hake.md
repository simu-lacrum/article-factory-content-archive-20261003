## 5. Hake

The oldest of the currently available Dota hacks. A true legend, but one that has not kept up with the technological race between other projects. The price is slightly higher than the previous two, without any additional functionality, but it is quite stable.

Let me list some of its advantages:

1.   Stability. The scripts are old, nothing new, but they are well-established.
2.   Age. The fact that the project has been around for about 15 years speaks volumes.
3.   An extensive set of user scripts that can be downloaded for free from the website (the quality of these scripts can rarely be compared to those provided by the top sites, but those who seek will always find what they are looking for).
