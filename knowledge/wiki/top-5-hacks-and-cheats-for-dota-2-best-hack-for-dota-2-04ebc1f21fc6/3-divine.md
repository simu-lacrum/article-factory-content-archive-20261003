## 3. Divine

A relative newcomer to the market, less than 5 years old, but still worth mentioning. At one time, it released some very interesting visual scripts, which are now, however, commonplace.

It feels like they have stopped paying enough attention to hack. The subscription is also quite affordable, but I would not consider purchasing it, as with all the other top members listed below.

Briefly on the pros:

1.   Low price. However, the quality is commensurate with the price.
2.   A standard set of scripts for heroes that work well, but lasthit, dodger, and automatic illusions leave something to be desired.
