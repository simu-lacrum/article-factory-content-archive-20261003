## Choose functions by use case

Do not pay for a long list simply because it looks comprehensive. Identify the category you need: visual information, cast assistance, hero automation, farming utilities or cosmetics. Then read the limitations. Our [mechanism-first explanation of Dota 2 cheats](https://cheatsgaming.com/games/dota-2/how-hacks-work-in-dota-2-4a2d06d5969e) shows why two similarly named features may work very differently.

Check whether configuration can be adjusted per hero, whether the interface is understandable, and whether there is a recent demonstration. A screenshot proves appearance at one moment; it does not prove current compatibility or low detection risk.
