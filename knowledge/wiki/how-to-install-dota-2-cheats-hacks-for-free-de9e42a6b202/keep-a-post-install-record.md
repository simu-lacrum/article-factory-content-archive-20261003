## Keep a post-install record

Record the product version, download source, license duration, activation date and files or services added. This is not an evasion log; it is a maintenance record that lets you identify stale software and remove it. Do not store a plain-text key in a public screenshot or shared note.

If the product becomes unavailable, stop launching it and read the provider's status message. Reinstalling repeatedly, mixing versions or downloading unofficial fixes increases technical risk. Wait for one confirmed release and follow its migration notes.

At the end of access, remove the software even if you do not plan to open Dota. Old background components and forgotten startup entries remain a system risk after the subscription expires.

Keep purchase and installation decisions separate. Payment confirms access to a plan; it does not confirm that your system meets every requirement or that the current Dota build is supported. Recheck the status immediately before downloading, even if the invoice was created earlier.

When support provides a replacement build, confirm it through the same authenticated channel used for the original delivery. Do not accept an executable sent by an unrelated account claiming to be staff.
