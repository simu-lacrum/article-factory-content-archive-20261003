## Use a low-conflict communication rule

1.   State one plan in the draft, then keep it short.
2.   Use pings for information, not sarcasm or punishment.
3.   Mute a player as soon as a conversation becomes personal.
4.   Do not debate blame while the game is active.
5.   After the match, leave the argument instead of carrying it into the next queue.

A calm loss with normal participation is not the same input as an abandon or a match spent attacking teammates. Winning helps the experience, but behavior score is not simply a win-rate metric.
