## A four-week routine

| When | Record | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before queue | Connection, time, mood | Skip the queue if any is unstable. |
| During draft | Role and one plan | Communicate once, then play. |
| During game | Mute triggers and abandons | Protect completion over arguments. |
| After game | Trigger and break | Stop after two tilted games. |
| Weekly | Conduct summary and history | Change one habit, not everything. |
