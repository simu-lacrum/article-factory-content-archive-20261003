## Choose sessions you can finish

Use a small hero pool and a role you can complete without forcing a complicated experiment. Avoid queueing while tired, angry, or distracted by a call. Two finished games followed by a break are more useful than six rushed games that end in an abandon or a reportable argument.

Do not switch to a purchased or “fresh” account. Valve has linked smurf accounts to associated main accounts; read our [smurf-ban recovery guide](https://dota2cheat.com/guides/dota-2-smurf-ban-guide) before treating account rotation as a solution.
