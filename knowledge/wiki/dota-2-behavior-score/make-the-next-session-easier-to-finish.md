## Make the next session easier to finish

Remove friction before you queue. Close software that competes for bandwidth, update the game outside your planned session, and choose a time when you can stay at the computer. If a crash or disconnect happens, record it and fix the underlying problem before starting another match. Do not use a network trick, account swap, or paid service to turn a technical problem into someone else's conduct signal.

When a teammate is frustrating, use one factual call, then mute. You are not required to win an argument to finish a match. If you notice that your own messages are becoming sarcastic or repetitive, stop typing and take the break you planned. The [private-lobby practice guide](https://dota2cheat.com/guides/dota-2-lobby-cheat-commands) is useful for learning a hero outside a high-pressure queue. Keep this routine for a full review window instead of changing tactics after one disappointing summary.
