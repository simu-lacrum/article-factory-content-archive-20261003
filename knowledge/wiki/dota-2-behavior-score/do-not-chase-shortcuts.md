## Do not chase shortcuts

Do not buy commends, account boosting, queue manipulation, or a “clean” profile. A coordinated exchange turns feedback into a target and may create additional reports or credential theft. A temporary number change is not recovery if the same conduct continues.

Likewise, do not expect a hidden queue, hero choice, or win streak to reset the score. Track inputs rather than checking after every game. Conduct summaries can update in batches, and daily checking creates superstition rather than evidence.
