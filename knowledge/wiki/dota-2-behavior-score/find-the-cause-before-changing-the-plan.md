## Find the cause before changing the plan

Review your latest conduct summary and match history. Separate connection failures, intentional abandons, communication conflict, and gameplay reports. Each has a different fix. If the network or power is unreliable, do not queue until it is stable. Starting more matches cannot repair a disconnect pattern.

Also check practical constraints: queue only when you have time for a full game, close downloads that affect latency, and restart the client if it becomes unstable. These steps do not promise a score movement; they prevent avoidable negative events.
