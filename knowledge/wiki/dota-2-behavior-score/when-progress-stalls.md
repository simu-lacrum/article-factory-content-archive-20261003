## When progress stalls

Reduce volume before changing the entire strategy. Re-check connection stability, mute earlier, and leave a longer gap between sessions. Keep notes private and factual: date, completed game, abandon, conflict trigger, and what you changed. Never publish teammate names or personal data.

For policy context, read Valve's smurf-account enforcement notice, then use the [Dota 2 account and conduct guide hub](https://dota2cheat.com/guides) to connect the related articles. Consistency is the measurable goal; no article can promise a specific score or timeline.
