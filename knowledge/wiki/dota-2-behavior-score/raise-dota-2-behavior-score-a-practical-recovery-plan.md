**To raise your Dota 2 behavior score, improve the conduct signals you can control.** Finish matches, prevent disconnects, stop replying to bait, and use one secure account consistently. Valve does not publish a formula that converts a fixed number of wins, commends, or games into a guaranteed score increase, so any paid “boost” or seven-day promise is marketing.

Use this as a four-week routine. It is deliberately boring: stable sessions produce better information than a grind made of abandons and arguments.
