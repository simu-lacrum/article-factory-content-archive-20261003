## Link-quality rules

- One Tier-3 article maps to one target URL.
- Use the target URL exactly once in the article body.
- Place it after approximately 35–65% of the article, never in the title, subtitle, opening paragraph, image caption, FAQ, or author bio.
- Use a descriptive partial-match or branded anchor. Do not use naked URLs or repeated exact-match anchors.
- Add a genuine bridge sentence that explains why the target is the next useful resource.
- Official technical references may appear in a short Sources section, but do not add other commercial links.
- Medium ranking pages should be framed as comparisons, not technical authorities.
- Medium setup pages should be framed as publisher-specific walkthroughs, not security guarantees.
- cluster.center pages may be framed as official product/catalog pages for current listed features and requirements.
