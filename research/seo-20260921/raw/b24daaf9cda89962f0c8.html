Aimbot & Targeting

  • Silent Aim (PSilent): Locks and fires on targets without ever moving your visible crosshair, spoofing only the camera position.
  • Normal Aimbot: Classic visible aimbot that smoothly pulls your aim onto the best target.
  • Aimbot Activation Key: Bind a hold or toggle key that arms the aimbot only when you want it.
  • Targeting Cone (FOV): Sets the on-screen angle within which enemies can be acquired.
  • Target Priority: Choose how the best target is picked: minimal FOV, closest, lowest/highest HP, threat, or random.
  • Aim Smoothing & Humanization: Frame-independent smoothing with organic drift, inertia, reaction delay, curved flicks, and stickiness for a natural feel.
  • Hitbox Selection: Per-bone control over which body zones (head, neck, torso, arms, legs) are targeted.
  • Headshot Limiter: Caps your headshot percentage and shows a live headshot-rate indicator.
  • Hit Chance & No Spread: Only fires when hit probability is high enough, with an optional spread-removal assist.
  • Distance-Scaled FOV: Automatically tightens the aim cone as targets get farther away.
  • FOV Circle Display: Draws and styles the aim cone as a screen circle or a world ring at target distance, with color, fill, and thickness controls.
  • Off-Screen Target Indicators: Points to targets outside your view and shows target distance.
  • Target Ignore Filters: Skips unstoppable, invisible, out-of-sight, or zipline-protected targets so you only lock what matters.
  • Backtrack: Fires at a target's recent past position so the server's lag-compensation rewind lands the shot.
  • Target Lock: Holds your aim on a single chosen target instead of switching.

Combat & Abilities

  • Soul Triggerbot: Automatically fires the instant a soul orb is on your crosshair, with range and delay tuning.
  • Ability Aim Assist: Auto-aims skill-shot abilities at the best target within their own cast range and cone.
  • Item Aim Assist: Auto-targets aimed items with dedicated cone, range, priority, and ignore rules.
  • Counter Spell: Automatically counters incoming enemy abilities.
  • Anti-Parry: Prevents your melee from being parried by the enemy.
  • Melee Faker: Fakes a melee attack to bait and cancel, including item-cancel options.
  • Heavy Melee Assist: Automates the charged heavy-melee swing.
  • Auto Parry: Parries incoming melee threats including heavy, light, Guardian, and Viscous puddle punch, timed to impact.
  • Auto Dodge: Reactively dodges incoming threats with an escape or down-dash and auto-counterspells.
  • Auto Active Reload: Hits the active-reload timing window automatically.

Visual ESP

  • Enemy ESP: Reveals enemy players through walls with fully configurable overlays.
  • Ally ESP: Shows the same rich overlays for teammates.
  • Info Elements: Toggle name, health number, health bar, hero icon, ability cooldowns, skeleton, and distance per player.
  • Box Styles: Draw players with 2D, corner, or 3D boxes.
  • Snaplines & View Lines: Draw a line to each player and show their aim direction.
  • State Color Overrides: Recolor players when invisible, unstoppable, or using an ultimate.
  • NPC & Trooper ESP: Show enemy/friendly troopers and neutral NPCs with names, health, and boxes.
  • Soul ESP: Highlights soul orbs in a custom color.
  • Objective & Object ESP: Marks breakables, jungle camps, power-ups, and enemy deployables (Venator traps, Graves skulls, McGinnis turrets).
  • Camp Respawn Timers: Countdown for cleared T1/T2/T3 camps and Sinner's Sacrifice, on the minimap or in-world with color gradient.
  • Power-Up Alarm: Early-warning alerts before power-ups spawn.
  • Threat Warnings: Talon bird locator, in-flight Bebop hook grab-range warning, and in-burrow indicator.
  • Footstep & Sound ESP: Visualizes enemy footsteps in the world and off-screen with directional arrows.
  • Minimap Unlock: Reveals the full minimap and overlays camp timers on it.
  • ESP Text & Icons: Controls ESP text size, image size, font, and drop shadow.

Chams & World Visuals

  • Player Chams: Render enemies and allies with solid, wireframe, or custom material styles, even through walls.
  • Custom Chams Material: Author your own KV3 chams material for visible and occluded states.
  • Glow / Outline: Outline or fill players with an HP-tinted glow of adjustable width.
  • Nightmode: Darkens the whole world without dimming the HUD, with tint color and strength.
  • World Modulation: Tints world geometry with a custom color.
  • Fog Control: Toggles and adjusts world fog density.
  • Skybox Changer: Swap or disable the skybox and set a custom sky color.
  • Custom Lighting: Recolor world lighting and particles.
  • Ambient Particles: Adds soft glowing orbs drifting across the map.
  • Anti-Blind (Drifter Ult): Blocks the screen blind from Drifter's Darkness ultimate.

Hero Scripts

  • Hero Script Engine: The single driver that powers per-hero ability automation across the roster.
  • Hero Automation Library (42 Heroes): Ready-made per-hero scripts covering the full roster: Abrams, Apollo, Bebop, Billy, Calico, Celeste, Doorman, Drifter, Dynamo, Fathom, Graves, Grey Talon, Haze, Holliday, Infernus, Ivy, Kelvin, Lady Geist, Lash, McGinnis, Mina, Mirage, Mo & Krill, Paige, Paradox, Pocket, Raven, Rem, Seven, Shiv, Silver, Sinclair, Trapper, Venator, Victor, Vindicta, Viscous, Vyper, Warden, Wraith, Wrecker, Yamato.
  • Ability Combos & Item Chains: One-key combos that chain a hero's abilities and items in the right order.
  • Auto-Cast Charged Skill-Shots: Automatically charges and releases charged skill-shots at the ideal moment.
  • Head-Lock Shots: Locks aimed hitscan shots to the head while an empowering buff is live (e.g. Venator crossbow, Holliday Crackshot).
  • Auto-Snipe (Vindicta): Dedicated auto-snipe assist for Vindicta's Assassinate.
  • Can-Kill Badge: Marks every enemy you can currently execute with a customizable ult badge.
  • Script Aim & Targeting: Dedicated aim mode, reach, smoothing, and headshot settings for script and ability casts, separate from the gun aimbot.
  • Per-Hero Overrides: Give any hero its own aim, targeting, and ignore rules instead of the global defaults.
  • Hero Script Visuals: Toggle a hero's script rings, target/kill markers, and indicators.

Movement & Automation

  • Auto Wall Bounce / Corner Boost: Automatically executes corner boosts, with a real-corners-only filter and wall visualizer.
  • Bunny Hop: Auto-jumps to keep your momentum chained.
  • Auto Strafe: Steers your air movement for optimal strafe speed.
  • Auto Wall Jump: Fires wall jumps at the right moment automatically.
  • Slow Walk: Scales movement down for silent, precise positioning.
  • Auto Slide: Holds a slide whenever one is available.
  • Auto Dash-Jump: Triggers the dash-jump inside the game's own dash-jump window.
  • Zipline Momentum: Conserves zipline speed and controls eject velocity.
  • Movement Recorder: Records and replays movement paths with an on-screen path preview.
  • Auto-Steer Assist: Experimental auto-steering toward the best bounce line.

Lua Scripting

  • AvaLua Script Engine: In-house Lua host for running custom user scripts with hot-reload.
  • Script Marketplace: Browse, publish, and download community scripts in-client.
  • Code Editor & Console: Built-in script editor with IntelliSense and a live console.
  • Rendering & Menu API: Draw, Render, shader, ImGui, and menu-building bindings for custom overlays and UI.
  • Game & Entity API: Read entities, game state, schema, traces, and prediction from script.
  • Aim & Combat API: Script access to the aim engine, combat, combos, backtrack, and hero config.
  • Input & Events API: Bind input, callbacks, hooks, and timers.
  • Networking & Storage API: HTTP, WebSocket, JSON, protobuf, file, memory, and persistent storage bindings.
  • ESP & Particle API: Script-driven ESP elements and particle effects.
  • Panorama & FFI API: Interact with the game's Panorama UI and call native functions directly.

Misc & Utility

  • Streamer Mode: Hides names, teams, avatars, match ID, and other identifying info for safe streaming.
  • Spectator List: On-screen panel showing who is spectating you, with hero portraits.
  • Bind Island: A panel listing your active binds, hotkeys, and running hero scripts.
  • Headshot Meter: HUD readout of your headshot rate with recent-shot history.
  • Override FOV: Sets a custom camera field-of-view angle.
  • Preserve Killfeed: Keeps the killfeed showing your own kills and assists.
  • Watchlist Monitor: Flags and tracks specific players across matches.
  • Disable Ping-on-Invis: Stops the game from pinging you when invisible.
  • HUD Scale & Opacity: Global size and transparency controls for every on-screen panel.

Config & UI

  • Menu Hotkeys: Bind the menu open/close toggle and an instant panic-hide key.
  • Menu Scale & Accent: Set the menu window size and primary accent color.
  • Menu Font: Choose the menu's body typeface from curated faces.
  • Language / Localization: Switch the menu language across 12 supported languages.
  • Menu Animations & Sound: Smooth transitions, animated snow background, and hover/click sound volume.
  • Render Quality: Anti-aliased lines and fills, plus ESP text shadow tuning.
  • Cloud Config Sync: Cloud-first saving and loading of your settings, no local files required.
  • Config Profiles: Save, load, and reset complete configuration profiles.
  • Keybind Manager: Central store for all feature keybinds.
