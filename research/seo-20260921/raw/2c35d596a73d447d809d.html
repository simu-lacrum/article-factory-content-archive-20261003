What the maphack (Info Screen) shows

Info Screen is a large package of visual features in the cheat's main menu. It's not one checkbox but a catalog of several dozen toggles, split into meaningful groups.

Map Hack — what's hidden by the fog of war

  • Teleports — the opponent's teleport arrows on the minimap (only you see them).
  • Jungle MapHack — reveals who's farming the jungle / neutral camps in the fog and where they move; the unit's approximate HP.
  • Ability MapHack — shows enemy ability usage through the fog of war: an ability icon where it was cast (on the map and minimap).
  • Fog of War ESP — highlights the nearest enemy hidden in the fog.
  • Show Illusion — a separate color for illusions and the original.
  • Hidden Spells — displaying hidden cast abilities in the fog of war.
  • Linear Projectiles — projectile flight lines through the fog.
  • Glyph and Scan CD — the cooldown of the enemy's Scan and tower Glyph.
  • Bounty runes in world — the bounty-rune icon through the fog of war.

Roshan + rune timers

  • Roshan HP on Screen — a panel with the boss's HP.
  • Roshan HP in World — an indicator right in the pit.
  • Rune Timers — notifications for the spawn of bounty, side and Power runes.
  • Camp Stack — a reminder of the camp-stacking timing.

Ward Tracker

  • Drawing enemy wards in the world: as a 2D icon, a 3D model or both at once.
  • Each ward's vision radius (Render range particle).
  • Visible by Sentry — where the enemy sentries are looking.
  • Visible by Enemy — a "the enemy or their ward sees you" indicator.

Hero Bars and Top Display

  • An HP / Mana / Ultimate bar above the heroes.
  • Top Display — a top panel with HP, MP, ultimates, a picked-rune timer and creep stats.
  • Optionally hide the panel by holding ALT.
  • Modifiers — the duration of Hex, Orchid, BKB, Aphotic and other buffs/debuffs.
  • Items panel — the inventory of enemies and allies next to the model.
  • Networth Panel — each hero's value in numbers.

Extras

  • Camera Distance — an increased camera distance with a smooth zoom.
  • Dangerous Ability Timers — a timer for the ultimate of Mirana, Nyx and other dangerous casts.
  • Tower Radius / Watchers Radius — an outline of enemy towers and watch towers.
  • Show Linken — a text or particle indicator of an active Linken's Sphere.
  • Side Notifications — notifications about purchases, teleports and ultimate usage.
  • Reveal Delay — a humanizing delay before revealed information appears on screen, so your reaction looks more natural.
