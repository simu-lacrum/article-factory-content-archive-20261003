1. Read the window

One death time, two useful marks

DeathWrite it downThis starts the calculation.
+8:00May be aliveThe random window is open.
+11:00Guaranteed aliveUnless he was killed again.

Dota's minimap status shows whether Roshan is alive, dead, or may be alive, plus the active pit. Use that display and actual vision instead of treating the earliest mark like a guaranteed spawn.

2. Prepare the map

Run four checks before the window opens

  1. 01

    Waves

    Which lane exposes the move? Fix it before your team disappears.

  2. 02

    Vision

    Can you see the useful entrances, and can the enemy see your setup?

  3. 03

    Team

    Who can arrive, who must show, and which major resources are unavailable?

  4. 04

    Decision

    Take, contest, bait, or trade. Say the choice before someone walks into the pit.

The useful call

“Roshan may be up at 31:20. Push top, ward the entrances, then decide.”

3. Practice it

A five-game Roshan drill

Every game

When Roshan dies, record the death and both ends of the window.

Before +8:00

Name the wave, vision, and teammate problem that must be solved first.

After the play

Score the setup—not only whether your team got Roshan.

A good cue can end with no Roshan attempt. A deliberate trade is still a decision.

Quick answers

Roshan timing FAQ

Does Roshan respawn exactly eight minutes after death?

+

No. Eight minutes starts the random respawn window; eleven minutes is the guaranteed end.

Which pit should I prepare around?

+

Use the active-pit icon and current map state. Pit preference has changed across recent patches, so a memorized single-pit script is brittle.

Can DotaSense know the exact random respawn?

+

No. The reminder tracks the known window. Confirm Roshan through Dota's status display, vision, sound, and team information.

Mechanics check

Built from Valve's patch notes

Valve documented the 8–11 minute window and minimap status in 7.38. The active-pit preference changed again in 7.41. This guide avoids a fixed drop table because those details are patch-dependent.
