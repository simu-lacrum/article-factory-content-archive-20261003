Why manual dodging is hard to master

The average adult human visual reaction time is 200–250 milliseconds. That is the hard ceiling: by the time a signal travels from eye to motor cortex to fingers, hundreds of clock cycles have passed. For most players the real figure is higher due to attention, pattern recognition and decision-making.

Skillshots like Hook or Sacred Arrow travel 0.3–0.7 seconds at a typical medium range. Mathematically the reaction window exists, but it is extremely narrow. Most players at Ancient and below statistically catch these projectiles regularly.

Automation removes three constraints at once: it catches the projectile in one frame (3 milliseconds), calculates the trajectory perfectly via a mathematical model, and picks the optimal save from what is available in inventory. Reaction time is around 100 milliseconds — impossible to achieve by hand.

What the module does

A scanner constantly tracks moving projectiles and point-targeted casts from opponents. When a projectile is aimed at you — trajectory calculation and activation of the appropriate save happen milliseconds before impact:

  • Blink sideways — exiting the flight cone, perfect against Hook and Skewer.
  • BKB / Manta / Eul — immunity or dispel against point-targeted ults (Doom, Reaper's Scythe).
  • Linken's / Lotus — passive saves triggered by priority order.
  • Blink-out — for heroes with instant movement (Storm, AM, QoP) the cast instantly removes them from the impact zone.
  • Priority engine — picks the save with the minimum resource cost. If a Blink dodge is possible, BKB is not spent.

Dodger settings in the menu

In the Defense section Dodger has a flexible panel — you can tune it per spell and make the behavior less obvious:

  • Dodger — the main toggle: auto-dodges enemy targeted projectiles and disables.
  • Per-spell dodge list — enable or disable dodging for each spell individually, plus Enable All / Disable All buttons.
  • Chance of Dodge — the trigger probability, so the reaction looks human instead of firing 100% of the time.
  • Min Stacks To Dodge — the minimum number of stacks required before reacting (useful for stacking effects).
  • Use Only One Dodge — dodges only once instead of chaining a series of evades.
  • Dodge From Your Own Projectiles — also dodges your own projectiles if they are heading toward you.

FAQ

Does it dodge Mirana Arrow at long range?

Yes. Sacred Arrow is visible immediately after the cast (even without vision, via position-prediction). Reaction via Blink, BKB or self-Eul.

What about projectiles under BKB immunity?

The system automatically distinguishes physical and magical damage. If BKB is already active it does not activate a second one — it reacts with Manta or Blink instead.

Will BKB be used on every Hook?

No. The priority engine assesses risk: at 100% HP with no threatening enemy items — it ignores the Hook. At 20% HP under double initiation — BKB fires immediately.

Is it compatible with Combo Builder?

Yes, auto-evasion has priority over any active chain. If a Mirana Arrow is flying during your full burst — the cast is interrupted to save you (if HP is at risk).
