Safety and spoofer

The main risk of using any cheat is an HWID ban — a block tied to your hardware identifiers: even after creating a new Steam account you cannot play from the same PC. Here is what both products offer:

  • Umbrella: Spoofer is built into the subscription, masking hard drive serials, MAC address, motherboard ID, and display identifiers. One-click activation through the Loader.
  • Melonity: Spoofer is a separate product — an add-on to the subscription. Functionally similar, but requires manual configuration and sometimes returns "device unverified" after a Windows update.

Umbrella cloud configs do not write settings to disk — the anti-cheat scanner finds no traces. Melonity configs are local (.cfg in the game folder), which more frequently triggers heuristic flags.

Patch speed after Valve updates
