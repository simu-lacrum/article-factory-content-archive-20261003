What is It?

Grenade tool is an interactive 2D tactical map with all possible grenade spots

In-game advantage

Oneway smokes

One-way smoke is a certain kind of smoke that allows you to see or kill a player without the risk of being noticed - the enemy simply cannot see you. This mechanic is one of the most useful and difficult to execute, but it also helps to achieve maximum advantage with low risk for the performer. Our tool contains a lot of oneways, which you can use and practice, to make difference in every round
