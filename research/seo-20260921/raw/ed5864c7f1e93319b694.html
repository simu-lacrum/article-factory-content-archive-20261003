Deadlock is Valve’s new hero shooter that quickly gained popularity. The game combines shooter and MOBA elements: gunplay, hero abilities, creep lanes, towers, and a souls system. Demand for Deadlock cheats is growing — let’s review what’s available and what to look for.

Deadlock Anti-Cheat

Deadlock uses VAC (Valve Anti-Cheat) — Valve’s user-mode anti-cheat. VAC operates in Ring 3 (user mode), has no kernel component, and relies on signature scanning and wave bans.

This makes Deadlock more lenient toward cheats than games with EAC, BattlEye, or Vanguard. However, VAC has an unpleasant trait — silent data collection: a cheat might work for weeks, then a ban wave hits. Valve is also actively developing VACNet — an ML system for detecting suspicious behavior, similar to the one used in CS2.

More about how VAC works in our anti-cheat overview.

Important Features for Deadlock

Deadlock isn’t a classic shooter — it’s a hybrid. This affects feature importance:

  • Player ESP — knowing all 12 player positions on the map is a huge advantage. Deadlock’s map is large with many transitions between lanes and jungle areas
  • Aimbot with hero support — each hero has unique hitboxes and abilities. The aimbot must correctly handle different models
  • Item/Creep ESP — highlighting jungle camps, soul deposits, urns. Efficient soul farming is the key to winning in Deadlock
  • Hero ability tracking — tracking enemy ability cooldowns (if supported)

Using Cheats in Deadlock

VAC — Deceptive Leniency

VAC seems like an easy anti-cheat, but its strategy of silent data accumulation is dangerous. A cheat might work for weeks — then a ban wave hits everyone who used a certain build. Recommendations:

  • Use a spoofer — even in a VAC game, HWID bans are possible. Spoofer catalog
  • Don’t play rage — VACNet analyzes statistics. Inhuman KD and hit percentages = priority review
  • Play on a spare account — a Deadlock ban may affect other Valve games (CS2, Dota 2) on the same account

MOBA Elements and Suspicion

Deadlock is a team game with MOBA mechanics. This creates additional risks:

  • Don’t farm jungle “blindly” — if you always know where camps spawn and arrive at perfect timing, it’s noticeable
  • Don’t intercept every urn — if you always end up in the urn’s path, it’s suspicious
  • Let yourself get ganked — if you’re “never caught,” teammates and opponents will notice

More tips in our safe cheat usage guide.

Outlook

Deadlock is in active development. Valve may:

  • Strengthen anti-cheat (add kernel component, like VACNet in CS2)
  • Add an Overwatch system (manual replay review)
  • Change mechanics, breaking existing cheats

This means Deadlock cheats will need regular updates, and choosing a proven developer (Mason, Phoenix) is an investment in stability.

Conclusion

Deadlock is a relatively cheat-friendly game thanks to VAC (user-mode). However, wave bans and VACNet development require caution. Mason Deadlock offers the best feature balance for the game’s current state. When choosing, prioritize ESP — in a MOBA-shooter, map information matters more than pure aim.
