# How to Raise Behavior Score in Dota 2

To raise behavior score in Dota 2, focus on the signals you can control: finish matches, avoid abandons, keep chat calm and use one secure account consistently. Valve does not publish a formula that turns a fixed number of wins or commends into a guaranteed increase, so recovery is a routine, not a hack.

> **Quick answer:** make the next ten matches easy to finish. Stabilize your connection, mute bait early, stop queueing when tilted and review reports as feedback rather than a score you can buy back.

## Find the cause before changing the plan

Start with the last few sessions. Were there abandons, disconnects, repeated chat fights, griefing reports or games started while exhausted? The fix depends on the cause. A player who keeps dropping from matches needs a technical routine; a player who argues every game needs a communication boundary.

Do not treat behavior score and matchmaking rating as the same metric. A strong win streak can coexist with poor conduct signals, and a quiet loss does not automatically make the score worse.

## Use a low-conflict communication rule

The simplest rule is to communicate information, not verdicts:

- call missing heroes once;
- use short objective pings;
- mute a player who is baiting you;
- do not litigate a mistake in all-chat;
- save feedback for a post-match note, not the fight itself.

You do not have to become silent. You have to make it harder for one bad exchange to become the story of the match.

## Choose sessions you can finish

Queue when you have the time and connection to complete a game. Check updates before launching, close downloads and avoid stacking several matches when you are already tilted. A small pre-match routine prevents more damage than a dramatic “recovery” trick.

If your connection is unstable, solve that first. Reboot the router, use a wired connection where possible and stop queueing when packet loss is obvious. Do not promise yourself that one more game will somehow repair a technical problem.

## A four-week recovery routine

### Week 1: remove avoidable failures

Play only when you can finish. Mute early, skip arguments and note every abandon or disconnect without excuses.

### Week 2: reduce friction

Use a small hero pool, a reliable party if that helps communication and a fixed stop rule after a rough session. The goal is consistency, not a highlight reel.

### Week 3: review patterns

Look for the same trigger: late-night queues, role disputes, missed breaks or unstable network. Change one variable at a time so you know what helped.

### Week 4: keep what works

Continue the boring parts. A calm routine that survives a bad game is more valuable than a perfect plan you abandon after one loss.

## Do not chase shortcuts

Paid “behavior recovery,” account swaps and claims that a third-party tool can raise your conduct score are not substitutes for the behavior Valve measures. Be wary of anyone promising a fixed number of matches or a guaranteed score change.

Melonity is the mapped Dota 2 product for CheatsGaming, but it does not raise behavior score or protect an account from reports. If you mention it, keep the relationship clear: a game-assistance product may be relevant to a separate gameplay discussion, while conduct recovery still depends on the player’s routine.

## When progress stalls

If the score does not move, check whether your sessions still include abandons, repeated reports or account-security problems. Use official support for account-specific issues. Do not buy another account or install a “recovery” file that asks for credentials.

## FAQ

### How fast can behavior score rise?

There is no public formula or guaranteed timetable. Focus on completed matches, calmer communication and fewer avoidable reports.

### Do wins raise behavior score?

Wins and conduct are different concerns. A win does not cancel an abandon or a report pattern.

### Can a cheat tool raise behavior score?

No credible source supports that promise. A third-party tool cannot replace the conduct signals measured by the platform.

### Should I buy a new account after a low score?

No. Use one secure account and official support. Account swapping can create additional policy and security problems.

### What is the first change to make?

Stop queueing when you cannot finish, mute bait early and fix any connection issue that causes abandons.
