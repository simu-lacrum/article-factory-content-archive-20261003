# Dota 2 Smurf Ban: Rules and Account Recovery

A Dota 2 smurf ban is not fixed by buying another account. Valve has said that alternate accounts can be linked to a player’s main account and that related accounts may receive penalties. The safest response is to stop manipulating matchmaking, secure one account and use official support where an appeal is available.

> **Quick answer:** stop using the alternate account, secure the account you keep, review the notice, submit one factual support request and return to a consistent one-account routine. Do not look for an evasion path.

## What smurfing rules are about

Smurfing is not simply “having a second Steam account.” The risk comes from using an alternate account to manipulate matchmaking, conceal identity or bypass a restriction. Treat the platform’s current notice and policy as the source of truth rather than a forum workaround.

## Why a purchased account makes the problem worse

A bought account can carry unknown history, recovery disputes, mismatched region details or previous enforcement. You also cannot verify what software or payment trail was attached to it. Swapping accounts after a penalty adds uncertainty instead of removing it.

## What to do after a warning or penalty

1. Stop using the account for matchmaking manipulation.
2. Secure email, Steam Guard and recovery details.
3. Read the notice and capture the date.
4. Use one concise, factual support request if an appeal path exists.
5. Do not send repeated emotional tickets or contradictory explanations.

Never give a third party your password or recovery code while asking for “unban” help.

## Smurf status and behavior score are different

An account can have conduct problems without being a smurf, and a smurf investigation is not solved by collecting commends. Keep behavior score recovery and account-policy questions as separate tracks.

## A realistic one-account recovery plan

Use the account you can secure and keep the routine boring:

- finish matches and avoid abandons;
- stop queueing when tilted or disconnected;
- keep communication short and factual;
- avoid account sharing and boosting;
- document any support response with its date.

The plan does not promise a reversal. It reduces new problems while you wait for an official answer.

## What recovery cannot promise

No guide can guarantee that a penalty will be removed or that a new account will avoid enforcement. Be skeptical of spoofer, “clean account” and instant-unban claims. Those offers often create a second security problem.

## FAQ

### Can buying another account fix a Dota 2 smurf ban?

No. It can add unknown history and security problems and may still be linked to the same player.

### Is behavior score the same as smurf status?

No. Conduct signals and account-policy investigations are separate questions.

### Should I use a spoofer after a penalty?

This guide does not provide evasion tactics. Use the official account and support path instead.

### What belongs in an appeal?

Keep it factual: account details requested by support, the notice date and a concise explanation. Do not send credentials.

### Can recovery be guaranteed?

No. A guide can reduce new risk, not promise a policy outcome.
