# Aimbot CS2 Explained: FOV, Smooth, and Target Selection

## Quick Answer

An **aimbot CS2** feature is a category of aim assistance: it influences how the crosshair moves toward an eligible target. TriggerBot does a different job. It deals with the timing of a shot after the crosshair meets a valid condition. That distinction matters because FOV, Smooth, target priority, hitboxes, and checks are not five versions of “aim strength.” Each label answers a separate question about where assistance can act, how it moves, and what it may consider.

## What Aimbot CS2 Actually Changes

Think of aim assistance as a simple relationship between the current crosshair position, a capture zone around it, and a pool of eligible targets. The feature first needs a target inside that area, then a rule for choosing between options, and finally a movement style. This is why a huge FOV and a different target priority can change the experience in completely different ways. One changes the search area; the other changes the choice inside it.

## Five Labels Worth Understanding

- **FOV** is the capture zone around the crosshair where aim assistance may consider targets.
- **Smooth** describes how gradually assisted movement approaches the selected point. It is about movement feel, not target eligibility.
- **Target priority** decides which eligible option comes first, such as the target closest to the crosshair.
- **Hitboxes** define the body zones that may be considered, including the head, neck, spine, hips, arms, and legs.
- **Visible, Team, and Flash checks** filter conditions such as line of sight, teammates, or a flashed state.

## FAQ

### What does Aimbot mean in CS2 software?

It means aim assistance that can influence crosshair movement toward an eligible target.

### What is the difference between FOV and Smooth?

FOV defines where targets may be considered. Smooth affects how gradually assisted movement approaches one.

### Is TriggerBot the same as Aimbot?

No. Aimbot concerns aiming movement; TriggerBot concerns the timing of a shot after a valid condition is met.

### Does an external product format guarantee safety?

No. “External” describes a product format. It is not a guarantee against anti-cheat action or account risk.
