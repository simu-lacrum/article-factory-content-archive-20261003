## How to read videos and screenshots

A useful demo shows the game context, the hero, the feature label and enough of the surrounding interface to understand the claim. It still represents one moment. Do not extrapolate a short clip into a current patch guarantee, universal hero support or zero-risk outcome.

The same rule applies to a product card: it can explain positioning, not replace independent verification.
