# Editorial Handoff

Verification date: 2026-08-28

This package contains nine standalone Medium drafts from the supplied T2 brief. The exact H1s, slugs, primary keywords, 160-character descriptions, required anchors, requested language mix, FAQs, and global visual sequence 01–18 are preserved.

## Required publication edit

- Give every article a substantive human fact-check and line edit before publishing. Preserve the required anchor sentence and description exactly.
- Medium's current AI policy requires disclosure for AI-assisted writing when applicable. Add the appropriate disclosure after the human edit.
- Caption every generated image as AI-generated in accordance with Medium's current policy.
- Recheck all changeable product claims—current features, supported versions, availability, service status, files, prices, and support instructions—against the official product source on publication day.
- Recheck any patch-sensitive CS2 or Deadlock wording on publication day.
- Do not add safety guarantees, operational installation steps, security-disable advice, or anti-cheat evasion detail.

## Research limitations

Live UnknownCheats and YouGame pages were not reliably accessible during research. An archived UnknownCheats visual-discussion page and accessible official/community forum reports were used only to identify user-reported symptoms and readability criteria. They are not treated as proof of current product behavior.

The external Medium comparison/install articles are included only through the exact anchors required by the brief. They are navigation sources, not verification for present-day product claims.

## Visual production

- Generate with Nano Banana Pro / `gemini-3-pro-image` unless the editor explicitly changes the model.
- Odd assets use the specified B branch with two named warm-story references and a 4/5 fidelity target.
- Even assets use branch A with Cluster violet `#635FD5` as a 3–8% semantic accent.
- B assets use `#635FD5` as the 35–70% dominant field replacing yellow or amber.
- All prompts passed their written QA gate at 92–94/100; inspect the generated outputs for text accuracy, brand-color role, unintended UI imagery, and reference fidelity before publication.
