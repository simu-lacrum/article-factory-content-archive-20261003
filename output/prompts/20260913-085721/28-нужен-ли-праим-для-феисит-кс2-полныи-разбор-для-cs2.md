### How does VAC work? There are two main types of anti-cheats in games: server-side and client-side, the latter having several levels of access. There are also kernel anti-cheats that run directly in the BIOS, such as Vanguard or Faceit. These are often cited as the best anti-cheats, but their design is also their biggest drawback.
