### ESP or wallhack

It basically renders enemies through walls, but it also has a ton of other uses, such as displaying HP, weapons, whether a player has a bomb or a defuse kit, how much time is left until the bomb defuses, what items they’ve purchased, and so on.

Cluster also offers a sound-ESP — it’s like a wh, if you want to play completely legit. It displays footsteps, which lets you justify how you knew the enemy’s location, even if you didn’t actually hear their footsteps.

### ESP

ESP is the visual-awareness part of a hack for CS2. The local feature list includes Box, Name, HealthBar, AmmoBar, Weapon, Chams, Skeleton, Bomb, and Defuse indicators.

Good ESP is readable. Bad ESP turns the screen into noise.

Start with only what you can actually use:

*   Box or Skeleton for player position;
*   HealthBar for quick fights;
*   Weapon if you care about peeking decisions;
*   Bomb and Defuse information for round decisions.
