## Red flags that end the evaluation

Stop when a seller asks for Steam credentials, hides the final price until direct messages, cannot identify the supported build or treats all downtime questions as confidential. The same applies to copied testimonials with no date, screenshots from another product and support channels that cannot be verified from the listing.

Quality is also visible in restraint. A credible page can say that a module is temporarily unavailable, that a hero is unsupported or that no safety guarantee exists. Those limitations help a buyer make a decision; pretending that every feature always works does not.

Make the final choice from the listing itself, not a reseller's chat summary. Save the plan terms and status date you relied on. If the price, duration or delivery method changes between the product page and invoice, stop before payment and ask for a corrected order.
