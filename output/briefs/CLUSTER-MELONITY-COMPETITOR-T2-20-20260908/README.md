## Editorial position

These are comparison and alternatives articles, not disguised advertorials. Each article must:

- disclose that the linked destination is one of the products being compared;
- define its comparison method before giving a verdict;
- acknowledge at least one verifiable strength of every active competitor discussed;
- separate vendor claims from independently observable facts;
- use “not publicly verified” instead of guessing;
- date-check prices, trials, product status, operating-system support and changelogs on publication day;
- avoid operational instructions for injection, bypass, evasion, process hiding, drivers, memory access or disabling security controls;
- avoid “safe”, “undetected”, “ban-proof” or similar wording as an editorial conclusion.

The final text should sound like an editor who has inspected the pages, not a template producing twenty permutations. Openings, section rhythm, examples and conclusions are deliberately different in every brief.
